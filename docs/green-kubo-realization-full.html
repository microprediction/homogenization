Realizing several directed Green–Kubo features

Every real matrix with positive-definite symmetric part is the exact directed Green–Kubo block of a finite irreducible chain with suitable features. Cycles sharing one hub give an additive state-count bound; for a uniform small-skew range they attain the rank-minimal count.

Constructive theorem

Fix $K=G+A\in\mathbb R^{d\times d}$, $G=G^\top\succ0$, $A^\top=-A$ and $d\ge1$. Put $B=G^{-1/2}AG^{-1/2}$ and choose an orthogonal canonical form

$$B=O\operatorname{diag}(\kappa_1J,\ldots,\kappa_rJ,0_z)O^\top, \quad J=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad \kappa_j>0,\quad d=2r+z.$$

For each nonzero block choose any integer $n_j\ge3$ with $\kappa_j\le\cot(\pi/n_j)$. Then there is an irreducible row generator $Q$ on

$$N=1+z+\sum_{j=1}^r(n_j-1)$$

states, with any prescribed strictly positive stationary vector $\pi$ of that length, and a real centered feature matrix $F$ satisfying exactly

$$K=F^\top D_\pi(-Q)^{-1}_{0}F,\qquad \pi F=0.$$

The inverse acts on centered functions. In particular, if

$$\|G^{-1/2}AG^{-1/2}\|_2\le1/\sqrt3,\qquad\text{then }N=d+1\text{ is attainable and minimal}.$$

Use triangles for all nonzero blocks and one extra leaf for each zero direction; no chain with fewer states can have fixed-feature symmetric rank $d$. The norm condition is sufficient, not asserted necessary for a rank-minimal realization in dimension above two.

Build stationary flows, then choose the features

Take a hub $0$ and attach $r$ cycles of lengths $n_j$, sharing only that hub, plus $z$ distinct leaves. For cycle $j$, set $\theta_j=2\pi/n_j$ and give each oriented edge forward flow $a_j$ and reverse flow $b_j$, where

$$a_j+b_j=\frac{2}{n_j(1-\cos\theta_j)},\qquad a_j-b_j=\frac{2\kappa_j}{n_j\sin\theta_j}.$$

The cotangent condition is exactly $a_j,b_j\ge0$; at least one is positive, so every cycle communicates with the hub. Give each leaf edge flow one in both directions, sum these flows as $c_{uv}$, and define $q_{uv}=c_{uv}/\pi_u$ for $u\ne v$, with zero row sums.

Each cycle and leaf balances its flow at every vertex, so $\pi Q=0$. The connected shared-hub graph is strongly connected even when a cycle is one-way, proving irreducibility.

Let the two potential columns on cycle $j$ have values $(\cos(k\theta_j)-1,\sin(k\theta_j))$ at its $k$th vertex and zero outside that cycle. Each leaf potential is its leaf indicator; all potential columns vanish at the hub.

Collect these columns into $U$ and put $L=-Q$, $F_0=LU$. Then $\pi F_0=0$, $(-Q)^{-1}_0F_0=U-\mathbf1\pi U$, and

$$K_0=F_0^\top D_\pi(-Q)^{-1}_0F_0 =U^\top L^\top D_\pi U =\operatorname{diag}(I_2+\kappa_1J,\ldots,I_2+\kappa_rJ,I_z).$$

For one cycle, its two Fourier potentials give $n_j[(a_j+b_j)(1-\cos\theta_j)I_2+(a_j-b_j)\sin\theta_j J]/2=I_2+\kappa_jJ$. Off-block pairings vanish because potentials on different cycles or leaves overlap only at the hub, where they are zero.

Finally take $F=F_0O^\top G^{1/2}$. Congruence gives $F^\top D_\pi(-Q)^{-1}_0F=G^{1/2}(I+B)G^{1/2}=G+A$; the feature rank is $d$, since the displayed symmetric part is positive definite.

Using Poisson potentials is essential: directly placing the desired feature values on the separate cycles would not in general cancel their shared-hub correlations. The construction is exact and works for arbitrary positive $\pi$, not just a uniform law.

A rational rank-minimal example

Attach two triangles with forward/reverse stationary flows $3,1$, then a leaf with flow $2$ in each direction. For the five potentials given by the indicators of the five nonhub vertices, and $F=-QU$, one gets

$$K=\operatorname{diag}\left\{ \begin{pmatrix}4&-1\\-3&4\end{pmatrix}, \begin{pmatrix}4&-1\\-3&4\end{pmatrix},2\right\}.$$

Its symmetric rank is five and its intrinsic skew norm is $1/\sqrt{12}$. Six states are therefore both sufficient and necessary, including with the prescribed law $\pi=(1,2,3,4,5,6)/21$; the certificate checks every matrix entry in exact rational arithmetic.

Why several strong skew modes need a separate count

The lower bounds $N\ge d+1$ and $\|G^{-1/2}AG^{-1/2}\|_2\le\cot(\pi/N)$ need not jointly suffice. A concrete four-feature target is

$$K_*=I_4+\kappa\operatorname{diag}(J,J),\qquad \kappa=\cot(\pi/5)=\sqrt{1+2/\sqrt5}.$$

Both elementary bounds allow five states, but five are impossible; two five-cycles sharing a hub give nine states. Thus the true minimum is between six and nine, not classified here.

Here is the obstruction. For any five-state realization attaining the maximal sector $\cot(\pi/5)$, choose a complex Poisson potential $u+iv$ that saturates the numerical-range inequality.

For a directed flow cycle of length $\ell$, the quadratic form has Fourier multipliers $1-e^{2\pi i k/\ell}$, so its signed imaginary part is at most $\cot(\pi/\ell)$ times its real part. Equality with nonzero energy uses only a constant plus one oriented first complex Fourier mode; summing this bound over stationary cycles gives the five-state inequality and its saturation conditions.

Decompose stationary flows into simple cycles. Saturation forces any cycle with positive potential energy to be a five-cycle whose potential values lie in the first complex Fourier mode; hence those five values are distinct vertices of a regular pentagon.

A shorter cycle cannot then have zero energy, and would violate saturation because its cotangent bound is strictly smaller. Every remaining five-cycle must traverse that same pentagon in the same direction to saturate the signed inequality, so the total stationary flow is a single directed five-cycle.

Arbitrary holding times do not change its energy-normalized skew singular values, which are $\cot(\pi/5)$ and $\cot(2\pi/5)$, each repeated twice. A full four-dimensional feature basis preserves these generalized singular values under congruence, whereas $K_*$ has the larger value four times; this is a contradiction.

The certificate verifies the unequal cycle values $\sqrt{1\pm2/\sqrt5}$ under varying holding times and checks the nine-state construction. Floating searches for six-, seven- or eight-state realizations are not used to assert impossibility.

Scope and classical ingredients

This is a population fixed-generator, fixed-feature realization, not recovery of a given hidden generator, a finite-sample guarantee, or equality of finite-time path laws. Changing the feature embedding is allowed; it is different from the identified-set problem with a fixed known embedding.

The count uses fixed-feature rank, never rank created by integrating different maturity loadings. A positive scalar speed change divides $K$ by that speed unless the feature amplitudes are also changed.

The Poisson representation of Green–Kubo is classical; see Pavliotis (2010), Proposition 2.1 and equations (2.3)–(2.4). Stationary flow cycles are classical as well: Landim and Xu (2016), Lemma 4.1; the sharp sector context is Ohga, Ito and Kolchinsky (2023), equation (3).

The certificate combines exact rational identities with independent generator, Poisson, quadrature and realization consistency checks. Numerical matrix calculations are not outward-rounded enclosures, and no priority claim is made for the classical flow or sector machinery.
