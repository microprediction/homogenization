Mean waiting time under fast switching

Strict speed order for every hidden chain; stronger signed bounds under reversibility.

Start a finite irreducible regime chain in its stationary law $\pi$, with generator $mQ$ and positive statewise total event hazard $d_i$. A fresh independent exponential trigger defines the first-event time $\tau_m$; write $D=\operatorname{diag}d$ and $\bar d=\pi d$.

$$W(m)=\mathbb E_\pi\tau_m=\pi(D-mQ)^{-1}\mathbf1.$$

For every finite irreducible $Q$ and nonconstant hazard vector, $W'(m)<0$ at every $m>0$. Reversibility strengthens this exact speed order to a positive spectral representation:

$$W(m)=\frac1{\bar d}+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{w_k}{1+m\lambda_k}, \qquad \lambda_k>0,\quad w_k\geq0.$$

For nonconstant hazards it is strictly decreasing and strictly convex, with alternating derivatives of every order. Its endpoints are $W(0)=\pi(1/d)$ and $W(\infty)=1/\bar d$.

A reversible, all-speed Green–Kubo bound

Under reversibility put $H=-Q^\#$, $f=d-\bar d\mathbf1$, $A_1=\langle f,Hf\rangle_\pi/\bar d^2$ and $A_2=\sum_kw_k/\lambda_k^2$. For every $m>0$,

$$0\leq\frac1{\bar d}+\frac{A_1}{m}-W(m)\leq\frac{A_2}{m^2}.$$

The full note constructs the spectrum and proves alternating remainder bounds to any order. For a reversible three-state benchmark it gives $W(1)=0.5585585586$, $A_1=0.125$ and $A_2=0.1423611111$.

Without reversibility the full note gives an explicit unsigned all-speed bound $|W(m)-1/\bar d-A_1/m|\leq c_2/m^2$. A directed six-cycle proves that the reversible remainder sign can fail.

Initialization and reversibility matter

This is waiting from an observation time, not an event epoch. The event-stationary regime prior $\alpha_i=\pi_i d_i/\bar d$ instead gives mean interarrival time exactly $1/\bar d$ at every speed.

A stationary directed three-cycle has $W''(0.01)=-0.0401230539$, so convexity can fail. The mean also stays exactly unchanged under time reversal, so it cannot replace the cause-label reversal witness.

Scope: positive constant statewise hazards and stationary initialization, not switched CIR intensities or arbitrary starting priors. The speed theorem does not assert finite-horizon survival ordering.

Certificate: 56 reversible chains in dimensions 2 through 8, independent resolvent derivatives, signed bounds, and an exact rational nonreversible counterexample; final line PASS. The full note adds the all-chain proof, exact reversibilization comparison and a separate nonreversible certificate.
