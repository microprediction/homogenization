Competing causes: exact remainder and prior correction

Fix a finite irreducible row generator $Q$, its stationary law $\pi$, and nonnegative cause hazards $h_r$ with total $d_i>0$ in every state. Conditional on the regime path, independent Cox triggers compete to produce the first cause.

$$D=\operatorname{diag}d,\quad \bar d=\pi d,\quad p_{\nu r}(m)=\nu(D-mQ)^{-1}h_r,\quad p_{0r}=\frac{\pi h_r}{\bar d}.$$

The killed semigroup integrated against $h_r$ proves the exact probability formula; positive minimum total hazard ensures absorption and integrability. The phase-type and event/time-stationarity background is classical; see Asanjarani and Nazarathy (2019), Proposition 2.2.

Remove the zero mode before expanding

All inner products and norms below are in $L^2(\pi)$. Define

$$C=D^{-1/2}(-Q)D^{-1/2},\qquad v_0=\frac{D^{1/2}\mathbf1}{\sqrt{\bar d}}, \qquad P_0u=v_0\langle v_0,u\rangle_\pi.$$

Both $C$ and $C^*$ annihilate $v_0$, so $v_0^\perp$ is invariant. The symmetric part of $-Q$ is the additive-reversibilization Dirichlet form, with kernel exactly the constants; thus the symmetric part of $C$ is strictly positive on $v_0^\perp$.

Let $J$ be the inverse of $C$ on that complement, extended by zero on $v_0$. It can be computed without spectral decomposition as $J=(C+P_0)^{-1}-P_0$.

$$x_\nu=(I-P_0)D^{-1/2}(\nu_i/\pi_i)_i,\qquad y_r=(I-P_0)D^{-1/2}h_r.$$

Factoring the killed inverse and projecting its zero mode gives the exact identity

$$p_{\nu r}(m)=p_{0r}+\langle x_\nu,(I+mC)^{-1}y_r\rangle_\pi.$$

The inverse $J$ is accretive: for $x=Cy$ on the complement, $\langle x,Jx\rangle_\pi=\langle Cy,y\rangle_\pi\geq0$. Consequently $\|(I+J/m)^{-1}\|_\pi\leq1$, by Cauchy–Schwarz applied to $\langle u,(I+J/m)u\rangle_\pi\geq\|u\|_\pi^2$.

Apply the finite resolvent identity on the complement once; it is valid at every positive speed:

$$a_{\nu r}=\langle x_\nu,Jy_r\rangle_\pi,\qquad p_{\nu r}(m)-p_{0r}-\frac{a_{\nu r}}m =-\frac{\langle J^*x_\nu,(I+J/m)^{-1}Jy_r\rangle_\pi}{m^2}.$$
$$\left|p_{\nu r}(m)-p_{0r}-\frac{a_{\nu r}}m\right| \leq\frac{\|J^*x_\nu\|_\pi\|Jy_r\|_\pi}{m^2} \leq\frac{C_r}{m^2},\qquad C_r=\max_i\|J^*x_{e_i}\|_\pi\,\|Jy_r\|_\pi.$$

The last inequality uses $x_\nu=\sum_i\nu_i x_{e_i}$ and the triangle inequality. The constant is explicit and finite for fixed $Q,d,h_r$, but is not claimed sharp or uniform over chains approaching reducibility.

The existing centered Neumann calculation supplies higher-order stationary expansions with a geometric bound when speed exceeds its matrix norm. This first-order result instead uses accretivity to remove that speed threshold and includes every initial prior; it does not replace the higher-order calculation.

Because the residual itself is linear in $\nu$, its exact worst-prior magnitude at fixed speed is

$$\sup_{\nu\in\Delta_{n-1}}\left|p_{\nu r}(m)-p_{0r}-a_{\nu r}/m\right| =\max_i\left|p_{e_i r}(m)-p_{0r}-a_{e_i r}/m\right|.$$

The original-chain coefficient and its stationary contraction

Put $H=(-Q)^\#=-Q^\#$ and $k_r=h_r-p_{0r}d$, so $\pi k_r=0$. Solving the first two orders of $(D+m(-Q))u_r=h_r$ gives

$$u_{0r}=p_{0r}\mathbf1,\qquad u_{1r}=Hk_r-\frac{\pi DHk_r}{\bar d}\mathbf1,\qquad a_{\nu r}=(\nu-\alpha)Hk_r,\quad \alpha_i=\frac{\pi_i d_i}{\bar d}.$$

These coefficients agree with the rigorous finite identity above, not merely a formal series. In particular, $a_{\nu r}=a_{\pi r}+(\nu-\pi)Hk_r$ separates the observation-time stationary coefficient from the prior correction.

$$a_{\pi r}=\frac{p_{0r}K_{d,d}-K_{d,h_r}}{\bar d},\qquad K_{u,v}=\langle u-(\pi u)\mathbf1,H\{v-(\pi v)\mathbf1\}\rangle_\pi.$$

This is the oriented fixed-feature Green–Kubo coefficient in the joint-default note. It is not a rank statement about maturity-integrated loadings.

Since $\sum_r y_r=0$, the coefficients and exact remainder sum to zero across causes. The first-order approximation therefore preserves total mass, but slow-speed truncation can still produce negative coordinates; the bound does not certify nonnegativity of that approximation.

The event-stationary case has an exact cancellation, not merely a vanishing first coefficient:

$$\alpha(D-mQ)^{-1}h_r=\frac{\pi h_r}{\bar d}=p_{0r},$$

because $\pi(D-mQ)=\pi D$. This statement assumes no event-triggered regime reset and a fresh future trigger independent of the initial regime; it does not assert the same cancellation for arbitrary priors.

Certificate and scope

The certificate checks direct killed inverses against the exact remainder, contraction norms, independent original-chain group inverses, simplex identities and changing-prior envelopes. It also covers an irreducible three-state generator with a nontrivial Jordan block.

It checks 42 random chains in dimensions 2 through 8, with three causes at 13 speeds, plus constant-total-hazard and zero-cause boundaries. The defective benchmark uses

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\\4&0&-4\end{pmatrix},\qquad \pi=(4,4,1)/9,\qquad [h_1\ h_2\ h_3]=\begin{pmatrix}1&.5&.5\\.25&.5&.25\\2&3&5\end{pmatrix}.$$

At $m=1$, a pure start in state 1 gives probabilities $(0.4187500000,0.3000000000,0.2812500000)$ and first coefficients $(0.2004132231,-0.0041322314,-0.1962809917)$. The all-prior bound constants are $(0.2478292046,0.1059233972,0.2485467717)$.

No diagonalizability or reversibility is used, and no lower speed threshold is imposed. The proof is an elementary finite-matrix accretive resolvent argument; the related symmetric/skew resolvent framework is in Duncan, Lelièvre and Pavliotis (2016). No priority claim is made.

The hazards are constant within each regime, not switched CIR processes. No finite-horizon survival order, conditional conformal guarantee or joint recovery of the hidden generator is claimed.
