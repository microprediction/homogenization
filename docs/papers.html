Papers

Write-ups on homogenization in pricing and survival models. The bibliography places them among the classical theory and the regime-switching literature.

Core

  • Cotton, P. (2001). An Analytic Approach to Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Processes with Fluctuating Parameters and Applications in the Modeling of Fixed Income Securities. PhD thesis, Stanford University. PDF · ProQuest. The origin of the regime-switching expansion on this site. The second half treats processes whose mean and volatility follow a two-state Markov chain.
  • Cotton, P., Fouque, J.-P., Papanicolaou, G. and Sircar, R. (2004). Stochastic Volatility Corrections for Interest Rate Derivatives. Mathematical Finance 14, 173–200. doi:10.1111/j.0960-1627.2004.00188.x · PDF. Vasicek and CIR short rates with fast mean-reverting stochastic volatility, and the corrections that change the shape of the yield curve.

Working papers & notes

  • Cotton, P. (2026). Survival Under Regime-Switching Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Hazard Rates: An Exact Reduction and the Corrected Homogenization Expansion. Note in preparation. Certificate: verify_expansion.py · demos: survival, convergence. The expansion to second order in 1/λ, checked against the exact two-state solution, with the orders of convergence measured.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). Got Milk? On Homogenization and Survival Probability. Article · code: homogenize. An expository account of the same model. Its first-order terms are incorrect; see the home page for the correction.

Related work on other sites

  • Cotton, P. (2026). An Asymptotic Correction to Luce's Choice Axiom for Competing Correlated Cox Processes. Working draft: PDF. Softmax as the homogenized choice law of fast hidden kinetics, with its first correction.
  • Homogenization without the corrector, on conformalprediction.net. Residual pooling in split conformal prediction is the averaged limit, stopped before the corrector.
  • Homogenization, on skaters.microprediction.org. An online width correction for distributional forecasts built from a hedged two-point scale mixture.
