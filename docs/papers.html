  • Green–Kubo Geometry of Joint Default (2026). Full note · Two-name CIR example · certificate. A positive CIR construction separates fixed Green–Kubo rank from rank created by heterogeneous maturity loadings: the sharp bound is $\operatorname{rank}(K\circ J)\le \operatorname{rank}(K)\operatorname{rank}(J)$, yet two regimes can produce a full-rank integrated correction for arbitrarily many names. For distinct CIR mean-reversion rates the exact criterion is $K\circ J(T)\succ0$ at every $T>0$ if and only if every diagonal entry of $K$ is positive, regardless of the fixed rank of $K$. When loading shapes repeat in clusters $C_c$, the exact rank is instead $\sum_c\operatorname{rank}K_{C_c,C_c}$, so full rank is equivalent to positive definiteness of every within-cluster block. For two names with heterogeneous CIR parameters, the loading Gramian is singular exactly when both $(\kappa,\sigma)$ pairs coincide: equal mean reversion alone does not collapse rank when vol-of-vol differs. For arbitrarily many heterogeneous names, distinct full parameter pairs always give linearly independent loadings, with no common-volatility or common-decay restriction. Expanding each loading in exponential harmonics and using prime-indexed harmonics isolates the slowest decay group even when rational decay ratios create collisions. Consequently the same exact cluster formula holds for unrestricted heterogeneous parameter collections. The resulting sharp regime-count inference is $n\ge1+\lceil\operatorname{rank}(K\circ J)/\operatorname{rank}(J)\rceil$; it reduces to rank-plus-one only for a single loading shape. When $d$ CIR mean-reversion rates coalesce on scale $\epsilon$, the integrated eigenvalues have the sharp hierarchy $1,\epsilon^2,\ldots,\epsilon^{2(d-1)}$, with explicit QR/LQ leading constants, so full rank coexists with condition number $\Theta(\epsilon^{-2(d-1)})$. For several distinct collision centers, the exponent multiset is instead twice the union of the within-cluster derivative orders; the largest cluster controls conditioning while all clusters contribute to the explicit determinant power. The required joint jet independence is automatic for CIR loadings: their time-Taylor coefficients form a polynomial basis in $\kappa$, reducing every clustered jet matrix to a nonsingular confluent Vandermonde system. At short maturity, distinct common-volatility loadings have the explicit eigenvalue hierarchy $T^3,T^5,\ldots,T^{2d+1}$, determinant order $T^{d(d+2)}$, and condition number $\Theta(T^{-2(d-1)})$, with QR–shifted-Hilbert leading constants. More generally, if $K=FF^\top$, $A_q=\operatorname{diag}(v_q)F$, and $s_q=\operatorname{rank}[A_1\ \cdots\ A_q]$, then exactly $s_q-s_{q-1}$ integrated eigenvalues have order $T^{2q+1}$; an explicit positive-definite block Gram matrix gives both the leading pseudo-determinant constant and, through its successive Schur pivots, every individual eigenvalue constant for arbitrary fixed rank. Distinct common-volatility CIR rates and positive diagonal $K$ force $s_d=d$: full fixed rank gives only $T^3$ powers, rank one gives the full odd-power ladder, and intermediate rank gives repeated graded powers. Here $s_1=\operatorname{rank}K$ remains the fixed Green–Kubo rank; later blocks measure rank created by maturity integration. At long maturity, for arbitrary heterogeneous CIR pairs, including repeated full-pair clusters satisfying the exact within-cluster criterion, the number of linearly growing information eigenvalues is exactly the fixed Green–Kubo rank $r$; if maturity integration creates full rank, the other $d-r$ eigenvalues converge to a positive transient complement, so conditioning still grows linearly. The inverse converges to the Moore–Penrose inverse of that complement, with an explicit rank-$r$ stationary–transient correction of order $T^{-1}$ and operator-norm remainder $O(T^{-2})$. The normalized determinant has a complete degree-$r$ inverse-maturity polynomial: its coefficients are the elementary symmetric polynomials of the stationary Schur-complement correction after whitening by the stationary block. This includes the cross-coupling through the transient block; the log-determinant follows to every fixed algebraic order from the corresponding trace-power series. The same stationary Schur complement gives the full matrix-valued inverse-maturity series: after $p+1$ explicit rank-at-most-$r$ terms its operator-norm remainder is $O(T^{-p-2})$, with a geometric bound once $T$ exceeds the whitened Schur-correction norm. The same inverse expansion gives a minimum-variance dichotomy: a budget or normalization vector with a transient-kernel component has a finite long-maturity risk floor, whereas a vector entirely in the stationary Green–Kubo range has minimum variance growing linearly with maturity. Thus exact finite-maturity rank does not imply that every exposure gains unbounded long-end precision. It also separates arbitrary-prior marginal memory from first-order pair dependence. Cause-specific first-default races identify cycle orientation: one unweighted multi-name race sees only antisymmetric row sums, while $d-1$ independent positive exposure designs are necessary and sufficient to identify every $d$-cause oriented matrix. The design-frame spectrum gives exact singular values and an explicit positive, closed-form reconstruction with quantified noise amplification. Among minimal designs the sharp condition-number infimum is $\sqrt2$; strict positivity approaches but cannot attain the equal-norm orthogonal optimum.
