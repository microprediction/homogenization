Papers

Our write-ups on fast hidden variables and their correctors. The bibliography places them among the literature.

Published

  • Cotton, P. (2001). An Analytic Approach to Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Processes with Fluctuating Parameters and Applications in the Modeling of Fixed Income Securities. PhD thesis, Stanford University. PDF · ProQuest. Vasicek short rates and hazard rates whose mean level and volatility are driven by a fast process, continuous or two-state; the reduction to linear systems and the fast-switching expansions.
  • Cotton, P., Fouque, J.-P., Papanicolaou, G. and Sircar, R. (2004). Stochastic Volatility Corrections for Interest Rate Derivatives. Mathematical Finance 14(2):173–200. doi:10.1111/j.0960-1627.2004.00188.x · PDF. Vasicek and CIR short rates with fast mean-reverting stochastic volatility, and the first-order terms that change the shape of the yield curve.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). Got Milk? On Homogenization and Survival Probability. Article · code: homogenize. An expository account of regime-switching survival probabilities.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). A Fundamental Theorem for Epidemiology. Article, 27 May 2020. Doubling times under a week against peak infection near 20%: de Finetti's theorem reduces the question to mixtures of independent neighborhoods, and the susceptible fraction at the peak to a harmonic mean.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). Addressing the Herd Immunity Paradox Using Symmetry, Convexity Adjustments and Bond Prices. Preprint. arXiv:2006.07341. Epidemics that turn over below the herd-immunity threshold implied by their early growth, explained by a growth convexity from Vasicek's bond formula and a harmonic-mean convexity at the peak.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). Repeat Contacts and the Spread of Disease: An Agent Model with Compartmental Solution. Preprint. arXiv:2005.10311. SIR with transmission attenuated by the probability that a contact is new; the speed at which acquaintances go stale changes final sizes by an order of magnitude.

Joint default

  • Green–Kubo Geometry of Joint Default (2026). Full note · Two-name CIR example · certificate. A positive CIR construction separates fixed Green–Kubo rank from rank created by heterogeneous maturity loadings and separates arbitrary-prior marginal memory from first-order pair dependence. Cause-specific first-default races identify cycle orientation: one unweighted multi-name race sees only antisymmetric row sums, while $d-1$ independent positive exposure designs are necessary and sufficient to identify every $d$-cause oriented matrix.

Working papers and notes

  • Cotton, P. (2026). Survival Under Regime-Switching Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Hazard Rates: The Fast-Switching Expansion to All Orders. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. The survival probability to every order in the inverse switching rate, with the initial layer and unequal switching rates, in closed form.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Fast Switching in Exponential-Affine Models: One Recursion to All Orders. Note in preparation. Summary · code and certificates. Any irreducible finite chain, including defective generators, driving any model whose state factors out of an exponential-affine formula: several regimes, two-factor rates and credit, CIR, jumps, counts, Heston, Bates, Merton and variance gamma.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Closed-Form Bond Options Under Fast Regime Switching. Note in preparation. Summary · code. Jamshidian's formula plus an explicit first-order correction, including the memory of the regime at expiry, in Gaussian integrals.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Three Numbers in a Yield Curve. Working paper in preparation. Summary · certificate · long maturities. To first order a yield curve sees a fast chain of any size only through a positive semidefinite Green–Kubo matrix, which bounds the curve's shape and leaves different chains indistinguishable; the long end is a convergent eigenvalue series, and keeping it exact gives a formula uniform in maturity.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). The Likelihood of a Fast-Switching Short Rate. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. The first-order transition density of a discretely observed Vasicek rate with fast hidden regimes is a Hermite correction with Green–Kubo coefficients, and its composite likelihood agrees with the filtered likelihood to first order; it identifies the same matrix as the yield curve, and its score test is a Jarque–Bera test with the kurtosis part projected onto positive excess kurtosis.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Fast Regimes in Any Pricing Equation. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. The first-order effective generator for any Markov pricing equation with fast switched parameters; the antisymmetric part of the Green–Kubo matrix enters through commutators.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Cumulants of Integrated Variance Under a Fast Regime. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. The fourth cumulant of a return under a switched variance is three times the variance of integrated variance only for independent Brownian noise, and that variance is its Green–Kubo term up to a remainder bounded uniformly in maturity; for a CIR variance with a switching level the same holds with the maturity replaced by an integral of the reversion weight, and the first-order rule gives every cumulant at fixed maturity.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). The Direction of a Regime Cycle in the Heston Smile. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. A chain cycling in one direction through variance levels and vol-of-vol moves the smile, an effect no reversible driver produces.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Switching Correlation: Correlation Smiles and Downside Co-Skewness. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. A correlation that jumps between calm and crisis regimes: a closed-form correlation volga in exchange and spread options, and a negative third cross-cumulant when correlation rises in down markets.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Portfolio Choice with Fast Regimes. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. Merton, the Markowitz frontier and a regime-blind investor with bull and bear regimes: each first correction is one Green–Kubo number, and observed-regime weights stay exactly myopic.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Pooled Conformal Residuals Under a Fast Hidden Regime. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. The conditional coverage of a threshold that pools residuals across a fast two-state regime: exponential in the horizon for a terminal score, algebraic for a path average, decaying at the predictor's own rate for a filtered state; calibration dependence at the sampling spacing as a separate, exactly computable effect.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Pulsar Spin-Down Switching and Timing Noise. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. Timing noise from a spin-down rate that switches between two values: exact two-state formulas, the Green–Kubo strength, and what timing data can identify.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Light Through Clumpy Media to All Orders in the Correlation Length. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. Transmission through binary and nested Markovian mixtures as a fast-switching expansion around atomic mix, with the Green–Kubo first correction and the radius of convergence.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Epidemic Growth in a Fast-Switching Environment. Note in preparation. Summary · certificate. Mean and typical growth of SIR and SEIR epidemics with switching transmission and recovery: the Green–Kubo gap between them, SEIR's missing first-order mean correction, and the effect of the direction of a regime cycle.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). An Asymptotic Correction to Luce's Choice Axiom for Competing Correlated Cox Processes. Working draft: PDF. Softmax as the homogenized choice law of fast hidden kinetics, with its Green–Kubo first correction and the arrival-order witness of irreversibility.
