Papers

Write-ups grouped by example. The bibliography places them among the classical theory.

Regime switching

  • Cotton, P. (2001). An Analytic Approach to Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Processes with Fluctuating Parameters and Applications in the Modeling of Fixed Income Securities. PhD thesis, Stanford University. PDF · ProQuest. Vasicek's model with a mean and volatility driven by a fast secondary process, continuous or two-state, and the expansions that correct it.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Survival Under Regime-Switching Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Hazard Rates: The Fast-Switching Expansion to All Orders. Note in preparation. Summary · certificates: verify_all_orders.py. The expansion in 1/λ to all orders, checked against a numerical solution of the two-state system.
  • Cotton, P. (2026). Fast Switching in Exponential-Affine Models: One Recursion to All Orders. Note in preparation. Summary · code and certificates: fast-switching. Any finite chain, several regimes, two-factor rates and credit, CIR, jumps, Poisson counts, Heston, and a continuous fast factor with correlation.
  • Cotton, P. (2020). Got Milk? On Homogenization and Survival Probability. Article · code: homogenize. An expository account of the same model.

Fast mean-reverting volatility

  • Cotton, P., Fouque, J.-P., Papanicolaou, G. and Sircar, R. (2004). Stochastic Volatility Corrections for Interest Rate Derivatives. Mathematical Finance 14, 173–200. doi:10.1111/j.0960-1627.2004.00188.x · PDF. Vasicek and CIR short rates with fast mean-reverting stochastic volatility, and the first-order terms that change the shape of the yield curve.
  • Fouque, J.-P., Papanicolaou, G. and Sircar, K. R. (2000). Derivatives in Financial Markets with Stochastic Volatility. Cambridge University Press. The fast-scale expansion for option prices.

Choice among racing processes

  • Cotton, P. (2026). An Asymptotic Correction to Luce's Choice Axiom for Competing Correlated Cox Processes. Working draft: PDF. Softmax as the homogenized choice law of fast hidden kinetics, with its first correction.

Conformal prediction

  • Homogenization without the corrector, on conformalprediction.net. Residual pooling in split conformal prediction is the averaged limit, stopped before the corrector.

Forecast calibration

  • Homogenization, on skaters.microprediction.org. An online width adjustment for distributional forecasts built from a hedged two-point scale mixture.
