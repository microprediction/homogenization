Renewal limits for unequal-scale staircase contractions

Commensurable horizontal scales turn exact phase invariance into a finite renewal equation.

Unequal base-power branches

Fix a base $b\ge2$ and ordered active intervals $I_r=[\alpha_r,\alpha_r+b^{-\ell_r}]$, where the positive integers $\ell_r$ have maximum $L$. Assume the intervals have endpoints on the $b^{-L}$ grid, the first starts at zero, the last ends at one, and their total length is less than one.

Choose weights $w_r>0$ with sum one and put $W_r=\sum_{j<r}w_j$. The corresponding continuous singular staircase is constant on the gaps and satisfies

$$C(x)=W_r+w_r C\!\left(b^{\ell_r}(x-\alpha_r)\right),\qquad x\in I_r.$$

For $\mu=\mathcal L(C(U))$ and the equal-mass cell-average projection $P_N$, write $E_N=\|C-P_NC\|_1=W_1(\mu_N,\mu)$ and $c_K^{(m)}=mb^K E_{mb^K}$.

Exact renewal equation

Aggregate weights by horizontal exponent, $p_j=\sum_{r:\ell_r=j}w_r$. For every fixed $m\ge1$ and every $K\ge L$,

$$\boxed{c_K^{(m)}=\sum_{j=1}^{L}p_j c_{K-j}^{(m)}.}$$

Indeed, branch $r$ is grid-aligned, contains $mb^{K-\ell_r}$ cells, and contributes $b^{-\ell_r}w_rE_{mb^{K-\ell_r}}$. Multiplication by $mb^K$ gives the displayed recurrence.

The equal-horizontal-scale identity is the special case $p_1=1$, when $c_K^{(m)}$ is exactly constant.

Limit and lattice phases

Let $g=\gcd\{j:p_j>0\}$ and $\mu_\ell=\sum_jjp_j$. For a residue $s\in\{0,\ldots,g-1\}$, put $a_q=c_{s+gq}^{(m)}$, $\bar p_j=p_{gj}$, $\bar L=L/g$, and $\bar\mu=\mu_\ell/g$.

Define the finite boundary forcing $r_{s,q}=a_q-\sum_{j=1}^{q}\bar p_j a_{q-j}$ for $0\le q<\bar L$. Then

$$a_q=C_s+O(\theta^q),\qquad C_s=\frac1{\bar\mu}\sum_{q=0}^{\bar L-1}r_{s,q},$$

Let $R>1$ be the smallest modulus of any other zero of $1-\sum_j\bar p_jz^j$, with $R=\infty$ if there is none. The remainder holds for every fixed $\theta\in(R^{-1},1)$; thus $g=1$ gives one scaled coefficient along $N=mb^K$, while $g>1$ gives one limit per residue class.

The proof uses the boundary polynomial divided by $1-\sum_j\bar p_jz^j$. Coprimality leaves $z=1$ as the only unit-circle root, giving the coefficient and the stated root-gap error on every residue class.

Exact cluster and phase laws

The complete set of subsequential limits along the fixed-$m$ geometric grid is

$$\boxed{\operatorname{Cluster}_{K\to\infty}\!\left(c_K^{(m)}\right) =\{C_0,\ldots,C_{g-1}\}.}$$

Every residue sequence converges to its $C_s$, so each displayed value is attained. Conversely, among finitely many residues every subsequence has a constant-residue subsubsequence, which rules out any other cluster value.

Consequently,

$$\liminf_{K\to\infty}c_K^{(m)}=\min_s C_s,\qquad \limsup_{K\to\infty}c_K^{(m)}=\max_s C_s,$$

and the full sequence converges exactly when all $C_s$ agree. The asymptotic frequency of the lattice phases is also exact:

$$\frac1M\sum_{K=0}^{M-1}\delta_{c_K^{(m)}} \Longrightarrow \frac1g\sum_{s=0}^{g-1}\delta_{C_s}.$$

Indeed, split the empirical average into the $g$ residue classes and test against any bounded continuous function. Each class has asymptotic density $1/g$ and its function values converge to the corresponding phase value; coincident $C_s$ combine their masses automatically.

Scope and certificate

This is an exact statement for a fixed multiplier $m$ and commensurable base-power horizontal scales. It does not describe arbitrary cell counts, nonlattice contraction ratios, or all singular-continuous quantiles.

The exact rational certificate checks 90 finite-stage recurrences for aperiodic and period-two dyadic staircases. Direct grids reach 768 cells.

The cell-error functional obeys $|E_N(f)-E_N(h)|\le2\|f-h\|_\infty$, while the staircase iterates satisfy $\|C-C_n\|_\infty\le w_*^n$ for $w_*=\max_rw_r$. Since each limit coefficient is a convex combination of its boundary values, its stage-$n$ approximation has rigorous error at most $2mb^{L-1}w_*^n$.

At stage ten this gives the actual aperiodic limit enclosure $[0.205672438933,0.213484938934]$. The periodic even and odd limits lie respectively in $[0.114151007537,0.145401007538]$ and $[0.186351699947,0.217601699948]$, proving that its true cluster set has exactly two points.

Erdős, Feller and Pollard (1949) proves the classical arithmetic renewal coefficient for positive power series. Here the finite-support recurrence and its boundary coefficient are derived directly from the cell projection.

This remains a marginal conditional-mean approximation statement. It is not feature-conditional conformal coverage, unrestricted optimal quantization, or a maturity-integrated rank claim.
