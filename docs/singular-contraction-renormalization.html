Exact renormalization for singular staircase contractions

Self-similarity preserves every base-grid quantization phase along an infinite geometric subsequence.

Equal-horizontal-scale singular staircases

Fix integers $b>d\ge2$ and active digits $0=a_0<\cdots<a_{d-1}=b-1$. Choose positive vertical weights $w_0,\ldots,w_{d-1}$ with sum one and put $W_r=\sum_{j<r}w_j$.

There is a unique continuous nondecreasing $C:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ that is constant on inactive base-$b$ intervals and satisfies

$$C(x)=W_r+w_r C(bx-a_r), \qquad \frac{a_r}{b}\le x\le\frac{a_r+1}{b}.$$

The level-$k$ active set consists of $d^k$ intervals of length $b^{-k}$. Their total length $(d/b)^k$ tends to zero, so $C'=0$ almost everywhere although $C$ is continuous and rises from zero to one.

Let $\mu$ be the law of $C(U)$ for a uniform $U$. Its quantile is $C$, while the plateau values carry all of $\mu$; thus this is a countably atomic target represented by a continuous singular quantile.

Exact finite-grid identity

Let $P_NC$ be the mean of $C$ on each of the $N$ uniform cells and put $E_N=\|C-P_NC\|_1=W_1(\mu_N,\mu)$. Then for every pair of integers $m\ge1$ and $k\ge0$,

$$\boxed{E_{mb^k}=b^{-k}E_m, \qquad (mb^k)E_{mb^k}=mE_m.}$$

In particular, the entire scaled sequence is exactly invariant under multiplication of the cell count by $b$:

$$bN E_{bN}=NE_N.$$

This is a finite-$N$ identity, not an asymptotic expansion or a statement along an unspecified subsequence.

Proof by cylinder decomposition

Every level-$k$ active cylinder begins and ends on the grid with $mb^k$ cells. It contains exactly $m$ cells, and a word $\omega=(r_1,\ldots,r_k)$ gives a copy of $C$ with horizontal scale $b^{-k}$ and vertical scale $w_\omega=\prod_{j=1}^k w_{r_j}$.

The $L^1$ projection error on that cylinder is $b^{-k}w_\omega E_m$. All inactive cells are plateaus and contribute zero.

Summing over all words gives $\sum_\omega w_\omega=(\sum_r w_r)^k=1$, hence $E_{mb^k}=b^{-k}E_m$. Equal vertical weights are unnecessary; the common horizontal ratio is the essential lattice assumption.

Persistent phases and failure of a coefficient

Write $c_m=mE_m$. The identity says $c_{mb^k}=c_m$ for every $k$, so every base value is repeated at arbitrarily fine resolution.

If $c_m\ne c_n$ for any two positive integers, $NW_1(\mu_N,\mu)$ cannot converge. More precisely, its set of subsequential limits contains the closure of $\{c_m:m\ge1\}$.

For the middle-thirds Cantor staircase, $c_1=1/6$ and $c_2=4/15$. The earlier two subsequences are the cases $m=1,2$; the theorem now preserves every $m$-cell phase along $N=m3^k$.

Relation to the special-BV theorem

The derivative measure of $C$ is singular continuous: it has neither an absolutely continuous part nor jumps. Hence the special-BV phase formula does not apply.

The exact renormalization identifies what replaces the special-BV coefficient in this lattice self-similar class. No claim is made for nonlattice self-similar scales or for arbitrary singular-continuous quantiles.

Certificate and primary context

The exact rational certificate checks 64 finite-stage identities for ternary and quinary two-branch staircases, split equally between equal and unequal vertical weights. The largest direct grids have $324$ and $2500$ cells.

At stage ten, the ternary constants are $0.166666666667$ and $0.266666669607$, with rigorous scaled-error enclosure radii $0.001953125000$ and $0.003906250000$. The quinary controls are $0.100000000000$ and $0.242424242464$.

Hutchinson (1981) gives the classical invariant-measure framework with arbitrary probability weights, while Berg and Krüppel (1998) gives functional-equation context for Cantor singular functions. The projection renormalization identity is proved directly here.

This remains a marginal conditional-mean approximation statement. It is not feature-conditional conformal coverage, unrestricted optimal quantization, or a maturity-integrated rank claim.
