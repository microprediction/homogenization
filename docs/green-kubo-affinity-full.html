State count and cycle affinity

The exact affinity budget for a two-feature Green–Kubo matrix.

This is an inverse-realization corollary of the cross-correlation inequality of Ohga, Ito and Kolchinsky (2023), equation (3), not a new general thermodynamic inequality. Their unicyclic equality case supplies the sharp construction.

Setup and sharp frontier

Let $Q$ be an irreducible row generator, $\pi Q=0$, $D=\operatorname{diag}\pi$, and $F$ a real, centered, two-column feature matrix. Write

$$K=F^TD(-Q)^\#F=G+A,\qquad G=(K+K^T)/2\succ0,\quad A=(K-K^T)/2, \qquad \chi=\frac{|A_{12}|}{\sqrt{\det G}}.$$

Assume bidirected support: $q_{ij}>0$ if and only if $q_{ji}>0$. For each simple directed cycle $c$ of length $\ell_c\ge3$, define $\mathcal A_c=\log\prod_{(i,j)\in c}(q_{ij}/q_{ji})$, and put $\Omega(Q)=\max_c|\mathcal A_c|$, with value zero when there is no such cycle.

For $N\ge3$ and $0\le\Omega<\infty$, realization on at most $N$ states with $\Omega(Q)\le\Omega$ is possible exactly when

$$\chi\le \cot(\pi/N)\tanh\!\left(\frac{\Omega}{2N}\right).$$

Equivalently, if $0\le\chi<\cot(\pi/N)$, the minimum maximal cycle affinity is

$$\boxed{\Omega_{\min,N}(K)=2N\operatorname{artanh}\!\bigl(\chi\tan(\pi/N)\bigr).}$$

The minimum is attained on exactly $N$ states with any prescribed positive stationary law of that length. At $\chi=\cot(\pi/N)$ no finite-affinity realization exists; a one-way cycle is the limiting construction. Above this value no bidirected realization exists even without an affinity budget.

The features and rates are free design variables: this is not a criterion for a fixed generator or fixed observables. It concerns one fixed-parameter $K$, not a matrix obtained by integrating different maturity loadings.

Proof of necessity

Let $U=(-Q)^\#F$, so $F=-QU$ and $K=U^T(-Q)^TDU$. The Poisson representation agrees with the integrated-correlation convention in Pavliotis (2010). Replace $U$ by $UG^{-1/2}$: its generator pairing has symmetric part $I$ and skew magnitude $\chi$.

Apply the cited short-time cross-correlation bound to these two Poisson potentials, not directly to $F$.

$$\chi\le\max_c\cot(\pi/\ell_c)\tanh\!\left(\frac{|\mathcal A_c|}{2\ell_c}\right) \le\cot(\pi/N)\tanh\!\left(\frac{\Omega(Q)}{2N}\right).$$

For completeness the last expression increases strictly with cycle length when $\Omega(Q)>0$. Set $x=\pi/\ell$ and $b=\Omega(Q)/(2\pi)$; the logarithmic derivative of $\cot x\tanh(bx)$ is $2b/\sinh(2bx)-2/\sin(2x)<0$ for $0<x\le\pi/3$. Indeed $2b/\sinh(2bx)\le1/x<2/\sin(2x)$; zero affinity follows separately by reversibility.

Attainment and the large-state limit

Put $\theta=2\pi/N$, $J=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $\eta=(A_{12}/\sqrt{\det G})\tan(\pi/N)$. On the cycle prescribe stationary forward and reverse edge flows

$$a+b=\frac{2}{N(1-\cos\theta)},\qquad a-b=\eta(a+b),\qquad q_{k,k+1}=a/\pi_k,\quad q_{k,k-1}=b/\pi_k.$$

Indices are cyclic and the diagonal makes row sums zero; flow conservation gives stationary law $\pi$. With $U_k=(\cos k\theta,\sin k\theta)$ one obtains $U^T(-Q)^TDU=I+(A_{12}/\sqrt{\det G})J$. Taking $F=-QUG^{1/2}$ therefore gives exactly the requested $K$, since $C^TJC=(\det C)J$.

Here $a,b>0$ and $\Omega(Q)=N|\log(a/b)|=2N\operatorname{artanh}|\eta|$. For fixed $\chi>0$, every feasible finite $N$ has $\Omega_{\min,N}>2\pi\chi$, and

$$\Omega_{\min,N}=2\pi\chi+ \frac{2\pi^3(\chi+\chi^3)}{3N^2}+O_\chi(N^{-4}),\qquad N\to\infty.$$

Expand $\tan x=x+x^3/3+O(x^5)$ and $\operatorname{artanh}y=y+y^3/3+O(y^5)$ at zero to obtain this formula. The remainder is a fixed-$\chi$ expansion, not uniform near $\chi\tan(\pi/N)=1$. Thus the infimum over all finite state counts is $2\pi\chi$, not attained when $\chi>0$.

Affinity is not an entropy-production rate

Under the stationary logarithmic-flux convention, define

$$\sigma(Q)=\sum_{i<j:\,q_{ij}>0}(\pi_iq_{ij}-\pi_jq_{ji}) \log\frac{\pi_iq_{ij}}{\pi_jq_{ji}}.$$

For $s>0$, the change $Q_s=sQ$, $F_s=\sqrt{s}F$ preserves $K$, $\pi$ and every cycle affinity, but $\sigma(Q_s)=s\sigma(Q)$. Hence $K$ alone gives no positive entropy-production-rate lower bound when both the clock and feature amplitudes are free. This scaling does not preserve separately calibrated mixing rates or fixed observables.

A physical dissipation interpretation requires additional model assumptions such as local detailed balance. The distinction between affinity and entropy-production-rate inference is already emphasized in the cited paper's discussion.

Exact benchmark and checks

A four-state cycle with uniform $\pi$, forward rate $3$ and reverse rate $1$, and potentials $U_k=(\cos(\pi k/2),\sin(\pi k/2))$, gives $K=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$ for $F=-QU$. Here $\chi=1/2$, $\Omega_{\min,4}=4\log3$ and $\sigma=2\log3$; three states cannot achieve this $K$ under that affinity budget.

The certificate checks the benchmark and clock scaling in rational arithmetic, cycle attainment under nonuniform laws, all simple-cycle bounds for random small chains, and independent correlation quadrature. Floating-point checks support the analytic proof; they are not interval enclosures.
