Homogenization across fields: a timeline

Averaging a fast hidden variable, and correcting the average with an integrated correlation, appears in mathematics, physics and fluids, finance, and biology and engineering. Each lane is a field, and each dot is a work in the bibliography, placed at its year of publication.

Labelled dots are landmarks. Hover over any dot for its annotation, and click to open it. The dashed lines mark the first Green–Kubo formula (Taylor, 1922), the averaging theorem (Khasminskii, 1966), and the arrival of fast averaging in option pricing (Sircar and Papanicolaou, 1999).
