All-speed competing-cause bounds

Any finite irreducible chain, any initial prior, an explicit error budget.

Let the hidden regime have row generator $mQ$ and stationary law $\pi$. There are finitely many nonnegative statewise cause hazards $h_r$, with total $d=\sum_rh_r$ not identically zero; put $D=\operatorname{diag}d$ and $\bar d=\pi d$.

For any initial probability row $\nu$, independent of the event triggers, the exact first-cause law is

$$p_{\nu r}(m)=\nu(D-mQ)^{-1}h_r.$$

Let $H=(-Q)^\#$, $p_{0r}=\pi h_r/\bar d$ and $\alpha_i=\pi_i d_i/\bar d$. The first coefficient and all-speed bound are

$$a_{\nu r}=(\nu-\alpha)H(h_r-p_{0r}d),\qquad \left|p_{\nu r}(m)-p_{0r}-\frac{a_{\nu r}}m\right| \leq\frac{C_r}{m^2}\quad(m>0).$$

The full note constructs $C_r$ explicitly from a restricted inverse. One constant works for all $\nu$, including priors varying with $m$; no reversibility or diagonalizability is required.

Zero-hazard regimes are removed by an exact trace-chain Schur complement, with the initial prior mapped to its entrance law. This preserves cause probabilities, not calendar waiting times.

The approximation and its exact remainder have total mass one and zero respectively, but the truncated coordinates need not be nonnegative at slow speeds. At the event-stationary prior $\alpha$, the exact cause probabilities equal $p_{0r}$ at every speed.

This extends the stationary Green–Kubo coefficient in joint-default geometry with the explicit prior correction $(\nu-\pi)H(h_r-p_{0r}d)$. It concerns constant statewise hazards, not switched CIR intensities, finite-horizon survival order or a maturity-integrated rank bound.

Certificate: direct killed inverses, independent group-inverse coefficients, pure-prior envelopes, simplex identities and an irreducible defective generator; final line PASS.
