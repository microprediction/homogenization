Cumulants of integrated variance under a fast regime

The fourth cumulant a fast switching variance gives a return, what its Green–Kubo form leaves out at every maturity, and the same for a CIR variance whose level switches.

The model

A stationary finite-state chain $y_t$ with generator $Q = Q_0/\varepsilon$ drives a variance $v_t$ in one of two ways: directly, $v_t = s(y_t)$, as in the switched Black–Scholes model of the any-equation page; or through the level of a square-root process,

$$dv_t = \kappa\,(\theta_{y_t} - v_t)\,dt + \xi\sqrt{v_t}\,dB_t,$$

as on the Heston page. In both cases the integrated variance, the martingale part of the log return and the log return itself are

$$V_T = \int_0^T v_t\,dt, \qquad M_T = \int_0^T \sqrt{v_t}\,dW_t, \qquad X_T = M_T - \tfrac12 V_T,$$

with $W$ independent of the chain and of $B$. Correlation between $W$ and $B$ is taken up at the end.

Parameters for the checks: the three-state chain $mQ_A$ of the any-equation page with volatilities 10%, 30% and 20%; and the Heston page's $\kappa = 2$, $\theta = (0.09, 0.02)$, $\xi = 0.4$, $v_0 = 0.04$, switching at rate $\lambda$ in each direction.

The quantity

The cumulants of $V_T$, $M_T$ and $X_T$. Given the variance path, $M_T$ is normal with variance $V_T$, so $\log\mathbb E\,e^{\vartheta X_T} = \mathcal K_V\big(\tfrac12(\vartheta^2 - \vartheta)\big)$ with $\mathcal K_V$ the cumulant generating function of $V_T$, and

$$\kappa_4(M_T) = 3\,\kappa_2(V_T), \qquad \kappa_4(X_T) = 3\,\kappa_2(V_T) + \tfrac32\,\kappa_3(V_T) + \tfrac1{16}\,\kappa_4(V_T),$$ $$\kappa_2(X_T) = \kappa_1(V_T) + \tfrac14\kappa_2(V_T), \qquad \kappa_3(X_T) = -\tfrac32\,\kappa_2(V_T) - \tfrac18\,\kappa_3(V_T).$$

The fourth cumulant of the return is not three times the variance of integrated variance. The drift $-V_T/2$ is random too, and its skewness and kurtosis enter, in small proportions.

A switched Black–Scholes variance

Let $C(u) = \operatorname{Cov}_\pi\big(s(y_0), s(y_u)\big) = \pi\cdot\big(\tilde s\,e^{Qu}\tilde s\big)$, with $\tilde s = s - \pi\cdot s$. Then $\operatorname{Var}(V_T) = 2\int_0^T (T - u)\,C(u)\,du$, and since $Q$ is invertible on mean-zero vectors, with inverse the group inverse $Q^{\#}$,

$$\operatorname{Var}(V_T) = 2TK - 2\,\pi\cdot\Big(\tilde s\,(Q^{\#})^2\big(I - e^{QT}\big)\tilde s\Big), \qquad K = \int_0^\infty C(u)\,du = -\pi\cdot\big(\tilde s\,Q^{\#}\tilde s\big).$$

The first term is the Green–Kubo term of the averaging page. The second is bounded for every maturity:

$$\big|\operatorname{Var}(V_T) - 2TK\big| \le 2\int_0^\infty u\,|C(u)|\,du \qquad\text{for all } T \ge 0,$$

because the difference is $-2\int_0^\infty uC\,du + 2\int_T^\infty (u - T)\,C\,du$, and each piece is controlled by $u|C(u)|$. With $C(u) = C_0(u/\varepsilon)$ the bound is $\varepsilon^2\int_0^\infty \tau|C_0(\tau)|\,d\tau$, so $\kappa_4(M_T) = 6TK + O(\varepsilon^2)$ uniformly in $T$, with an explicit constant.

A CIR variance with a switching level

Given the regime path, the variance is a linear equation with a known forcing, and its integral has conditional mean

$$\mathbb E[V_T \mid y] = \frac{v_0\,(1 - e^{-\kappa T})}{\kappa} + \int_0^T h(u)\,\theta_{y_u}\,du, \qquad h(u) = 1 - e^{-\kappa (T - u)} .$$

A level in force at time $u$ is felt for the rest of the horizon, less the part that has not yet reverted. The conditional variance $\operatorname{Var}(V_T \mid y) = \xi^2\int_0^T \mathbb E[v_u \mid y]\,\big((1 - e^{-\kappa(T-u)})/\kappa\big)^2\,du$ is linear in the path, so its mean is the averaged model's value. Hence, exactly,

$$\operatorname{Var}(V_T) = \operatorname{Var}_{\mathrm{av}}(V_T) + 2\int_0^T h(u)\int_0^u h(u')\,C(u - u')\,du'\,du, \qquad C(u) = \operatorname{Cov}_\pi\big(\theta_{y_0}, \theta_{y_u}\big).$$

Replacing the double integral by its Green–Kubo form $2K\int_0^T h^2\,du$ costs at most $4\int_0^\infty u|C(u)|\,du$, again uniformly in $T$: truncating the inner integral costs $2\int_0^\infty u|C|$, and replacing $h(u')$ by $h(u)$ costs the same, because $h(u) - h(u') = e^{-\kappa(T-u)}\big(1 - e^{-\kappa(u-u')}\big) \le \kappa(u - u')\,e^{-\kappa(T-u)}$ and $e^{-\kappa(T-u)}$ integrates to at most $1/\kappa$. So

$$\kappa_4(M_T) = 3\operatorname{Var}_{\mathrm{av}}(V_T) + 6K\Big(T - \frac{2(1 - e^{-\kappa T})}{\kappa} + \frac{1 - e^{-2\kappa T}}{2\kappa}\Big) + O(\varepsilon^2) \quad\text{uniformly in } T .$$

The switching term is the Black–Scholes one with $T$ replaced by $\int_0^T h^2$, which is $T - 3/(2\kappa)$ at long maturities: mean reversion delays the effect of the regimes by a reversion time and a half. All cumulants at fixed maturity

Polynomials in $(v, z)$ with $z_t = \int_0^t v$ of total degree at most four form a space that the CIR generator preserves, so on it every operator is a finite matrix and the moments of $V_T$ are exact (Cuchiero, Keller-Ressel and Teichmann 2012). The first-order rule applies to the same matrices: the switched level enters through $A = \kappa\,\partial_v$, and the correction is $K_0(\tilde\theta, \tilde\theta)\,A^2$, applied as a Duhamel term to the averaged semigroup.

Moments $a_k + \varepsilon b_k$ give cumulants by differentiating the moment–cumulant polynomial. The variance term reproduces $2K\int_0^T h^2$ exactly, and at one year the residuals fall at second order:

When $\kappa$ switches too, the coefficients that enter are the products $c = \kappa\theta$ and $\kappa$, as the any-equation page says, with $A_c = \partial_v$ and $A_\kappa = -v\,\partial_v$. These do not commute, $[A_c, A_\kappa] = -\partial_v$, so the direction of a regime cycle moves the mean of $V_T$. On the one-way cycle $Q_C$ of the general certificate the rule's residuals for $\kappa_1$ to $\kappa_4$ have orders 2.00, 2.02, 2.05, 1.96; the symmetric part of the Green–Kubo matrix alone leaves the mean with order 0.43.

Leverage

With $d\langle W, B\rangle = \rho\,dt$ the martingale $M_T$ is no longer normal given the variance path, and the identities at the top fail. Everything said about $V_T$ still holds, since $V_T$ does not involve $W$; only the transfer to $M_T$ and $X_T$ changes. Scope

The chain is stationary at the start and fixed, and the bounds uniform in maturity are for the variance of $V_T$ and hence for $\kappa_4(M_T)$. The higher cumulants of $V_T$ are corrected at fixed maturity, with a remainder of order $\varepsilon^2$ that may grow with $T$.

Moments of the square-root process exist whether or not the Feller condition holds, so nothing here needs it. Option prices under the switched level are on the Heston page, where the engine gives them to every order; the smile they imply at first order is the variance-convexity term of the any-equation page, the first-order form of Hull and White's mixing formula.
