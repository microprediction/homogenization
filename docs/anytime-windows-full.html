Anytime raw-window confidence budgets

One trajectory can be checked repeatedly and stopped when a reconstruction certificate passes. A single stationary-target moment event holds at every block count, from every starting state, without knowing the stationary law. The window design and a justified mixing bound must still be fixed before observing the record.

Setup and theorem

Let $Y$ have a fixed finite irreducible row generator $Q$ on $n\ge2$ known labeled states, with stationary law $\pi$. Fix $T>0$, $0<\delta<1$ and a certified Dobrushin upper bound $0\le\alpha<1$ for $P=e^{3TQ}$. Define successive path-block occupations and their two raw ordered moment estimates by

$$O_{kj}=\int_{(3k+j)T}^{(3k+j+1)T}e_{Y_t}dt,\quad Z_{ki}=O_{k0}O_{k,i+1}^T,\quad \widehat A_{i,N}=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}Z_{ki},\quad i=0,1.$$

The fixed targets are $A_i=\mathbb E_\pi Z_{0i}$, not transient one-block moments or moments centered using fitted means. Each reward matrix is nonnegative and its entries sum to $T^2$.

$$c=\frac1{1-\alpha},\quad L=1+c,\quad j_N=\lfloor\log_2N\rfloor,\quad \ell_N=\log\frac{4n^2(j_N+1)(j_N+2)}{\delta},$$ $$\eta_N=nT^2L\sqrt{\frac{\ell_N}{N}}+\frac{\sqrt2\,T^2c}{N}.$$
$$\boxed{\inf_x\Pr_x\!\left(\forall N\ge1,\ \forall i\in\{0,1\}:\quad \|\widehat A_{i,N}-A_i\|_F\le\eta_N\right)\ge1-\delta.}$$

The probability is under the specified Markov model conditional on $Y_0=x$. Integrating over $x$ covers every unknown initial law, including a deterministic start. For fixed design the sampling term is $O(\sqrt{\log\log N/N})$ as $N\to\infty$, with a dimension-free $O(N^{-1})$ boundary term.

A path-block Poisson martingale

Put $S_k=Y_{3kT}$ and $K_i(x)=\mathbb E_x Z_{0i}$, viewed as a matrix-valued function of the block's starting state. Then $K_i(x)/T^2$ is a probability vector on ordered state pairs and $\pi K_i=A_i$. The series

$$H_i=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\{P^rK_i-\mathbf1 A_i\},\qquad H_i-PH_i=K_i-\mathbf1 A_i$$

converges absolutely by Dobrushin contraction. Each scalar entry of $H_i$ has oscillation at most $T^2c$. Total-variation contraction, followed by the simplex inequality $\|p-q\|_2\le\sqrt2\,d_{\rm TV}(p,q)$, also gives

$$\|H_i(x)-H_i(y)\|_F \le\sum_{r\ge0}\sqrt2\,T^2d_{\rm TV}(e_xP^r,e_yP^r) \le\sqrt2\,T^2c.$$

Reveal successive complete path blocks and let $\mathcal F_k$ contain the path through time $3kT$. The matrix increments

$$D_{ki}=Z_{ki}+H_i(S_{k+1})-K_i(S_k)-(PH_i)(S_k) =Z_{ki}+H_i(S_{k+1})-H_i(S_k)-A_i$$

have conditional mean zero given $\mathcal F_k$; reward and endpoint need not be independent. Each scalar entry's conditional range has length at most $w=T^2L$. Writing $M_{i,N}=\sum_{k<N}D_{ki}$ gives the pathwise decomposition

$$N(\widehat A_{i,N}-A_i)=M_{i,N}+H_i(S_0)-H_i(S_N).$$

The boundary's Frobenius norm is at most $\sqrt2T^2c$ for every path and every $N$. The unknown $\pi$ and $H_i$ appear only in the proof, not in the computable radius.

Dyadic stitching, with constants

For any scalar entry $M_N$, conditional Hoeffding implies that $\exp\{\lambda M_N-\lambda^2Nw^2/8\}$ is a nonnegative supermartingale starting at one, conditional on any $S_0=x$. For any fixed integer $u\ge1$ and $\ell>0$, the maximal inequality therefore gives

$$\Pr_x\!\left(\max_{1\le N\le u}M_N\ge w\sqrt{u\ell/2}\right)\le e^{-\ell}.$$

Indeed choose $\lambda=\sqrt{8\ell/(uw^2)}$; at such a crossing the supermartingale reaches at least $e^\ell$. Apply the same argument to $-M_N$.

In epoch $2^j\le N<2^{j+1}$ choose $u=2^{j+1}$ and $\ell=\log\{4n^2(j+1)(j+2)/\delta\}$. A crossing of $w\sqrt{N\ell}$ in that epoch implies a crossing of $w\sqrt{2^j\ell}$ before $u$.

For each sign, entry and channel, this has probability at most $\delta/[4n^2(j+1)(j+2)]$. There are $4n^2$ sign/entry/channel choices and $\sum_{j\ge0}[(j+1)(j+2)]^{-1}=1$. Thus simultaneously $\|M_{i,N}\|_F\le nw\sqrt{N\ell_N}$ for both channels and every $N$ except on an event of probability at most $\delta$; adding the pathwise boundary proves the theorem.

Stopping and reconstruction

At each $N$, the budgets $\eta_0=\eta_1=\eta_N$ can feed the raw stationary-target inverse and residual-aware error certificate. On the simultaneous moment event, every candidate satisfying that deterministic certificate has its asserted reconstruction error, even if selected from these same observations. Consequently, for any block-boundary stopping rule $\tau$,

$$\Pr_x(\tau<\infty\text{ and an incorrect reconstruction is certified at }\tau)\le\delta.$$

The same event controls every report if several passing reconstructions are issued. A test failure requires abstention, and eventual passage is not guaranteed. This is not coverage conditional on reporting, nor distribution-free feature-conditional conformal coverage.

For fixed aligned left-endpoint grid occupations, spacing $h$ with integer $T/h$, the proof applies unchanged to the stationary grid targets. With a separately justified exit-rate bound $a\ge\max_x(-Q_{xx})$, the stationary mesh bound gives simultaneous continuous-target budgets $\eta_N+T^2h^2a^2/3$. This nonvanishing fixed-grid term is not removed by stopping or by collecting more blocks.

Checks and relation to existing work

The certificate verifies $54$ exact conditional zero-mean/range identities and $6$ exact matrix-diameter bounds in a dependent three-state block model. It computes $12$ exact finite-horizon first-passage laws and checks the epoch allocation algebra. Floating cross-checks cover $344$ continuous-time diameters on $13$ chains, including sparse and defective examples; these checks do not prove infinite-horizon coverage.

For the design $n=2$, $T=\log2$, $\alpha=1/8$, $\delta=0.05$, the anytime radius at $N=10^6$ is approximately $0.007076533$. This is a moment-budget benchmark, not an observed reconstruction or evidence that its sufficient tests pass.

Poisson martingale decompositions for Markov-chain concentration are classical; see Glynn and Ormoneit (2002), Section 2. Time-uniform supermartingale bounds and geometric-epoch stitching are also classical; see Howard et al. (2021), Section 3.1 and Theorem 1. The result here specializes these tools to the raw path-window matrices with a dimension-free boundary correction.

The constants are conservative and not uniform as $\alpha\uparrow1$. Selecting $T$, the grid, the labels or a plug-in mixing bound from the same record is not covered without additional simultaneous control. Hidden or noisy states, model misspecification and arithmetic error also require separate bounds.
