Finite duration bins identify Erlang-sampled generators

A finite-valued duration category can resolve exact generator aliases without keeping individual timestamps or choosing a matrix-logarithm branch.

The experiment and result

Let $Q$ be any finite time-homogeneous row generator on a complete known labeled $n$-state space, and start from a full-support law independent of the clock. Draw $T\sim\operatorname{Erlang}(r,\lambda)$ independently of the whole chain, with unknown integer shape $1\le r\le R$ for a known integer bound $R\ge1$, and unknown rate $\lambda>0$. Equivalently, $T$ is the total of $r$ independent rate-$\lambda$ exponential stages.

Fix any known bin width $h>0$ and retain only $(Y_0,Y_T,J)$, where

$$J=\min\{\lfloor T/h\rfloor,2R\}.$$

The $2R$ finite bins and one overflow category identify $(r,\lambda,Q)$ from the record's population law. This holds for reducible, absorbing and defective generators, without a rate cap, reversibility or simple spectrum. It is not recovery from a single observed record or a claim that $2R+1$ categories are minimal.

For $k=0,\ldots,2r-1$, the observed matrices are joint subprobabilities, not transitions normalized within a bin:

$$H_k(i,j)=\Pr(Y_T=j,J=k\mid Y_0=i) =\frac{\lambda^r}{(r-1)!}\int_{kh}^{(k+1)h}t^{r-1}e^{t(Q-\lambda I)}\,dt.$$

Full support of the starting law exposes every labeled row of the $H_k$. First identify the clock from $p=\Pr(J=0)=F_{r,\lambda}(h)$ and $q=\Pr(J\le1)=F_{r,\lambda}(2h)$, then use its first $2r$ bin matrices below. For known shape, one can take $R=r$ and $p$ alone fixes the rate.

Two cutpoints identify Gamma shape and rate

More generally, for any real shape $\alpha>0$ and rate $\lambda>0$, the Gamma CDF values at any two fixed cutpoints $0<u<v$ uniquely determine $(\alpha,\lambda)$. For two candidate densities their log ratio is

$$\log\frac{f_{\alpha,\lambda}(t)}{f_{\beta,\eta}(t)} =c+(\alpha-\beta)\log t-(\lambda-\eta)t.$$

Unless the parameters coincide, its derivative has at most one zero, so the density difference has at most two distinct positive zeros. Equal CDFs at $u,v$ would make the density difference integrate to zero on each of $(0,u),(u,v),(v,\infty)$. A nonzero analytic difference must change sign inside each such interval, forcing three distinct zeros, a contradiction.

For integer candidates $s=1,\ldots,R$, solve $F_{s,\lambda_s}(h)=p$, which is strictly increasing in $\lambda_s$ from zero to one. Exactly one candidate also satisfies $F_{s,\lambda_s}(2h)=q$ at an exact Erlang model law. Gamma quantile-ratio geometry is classical background: see Saunders and Moran (1978); the crossing proof above is self-contained.

The real-shape lemma calibrates only the clock; the finite matrix reconstruction here uses an integer shape. The known upper bound fixes the category count in advance, and no uniform shape-separation or finite-sample selection guarantee is asserted.

Explicit inversion from the finite bins

Set $d=r-1$, $A=Q-\lambda I$ and $B=e^{hA}$. Form the $rn\times rn$ block Hankel matrices, with zero-based block indices,

$$\mathsf H_0=[H_{i+j}]_{i,j=0}^{r-1},\qquad \mathsf H_1=[H_{i+j+1}]_{i,j=0}^{r-1}.$$

All the inverses below exist at every exact model law. Recover $B$ from a single block, then $Q$ by a finite difference:

$$B=\frac1r(\mathsf H_1\mathsf H_0^{-1})_{r-1,r-1},\qquad D=\sum_{k=0}^{d}(-1)^{d-k}\binom dk B^{-k}H_k,$$ $$Q=\lambda I+\lambda^r h^d(B-I)D^{-1}.$$

No matrix logarithm or root is taken, and no eigenvalues are matched across bins. Thus a large bin width does not impose a Nyquist-type rate restriction on exact population identification.

Why the inversion is valid

Translation inside the integral gives $H_k=B^kF(k)$, where $F$ is a matrix polynomial of degree $d$. Its coefficients commute with $B$, and its leading coefficient is

$$F_d=\frac{\lambda^r h^d}{d!}\int_0^h e^{uA}\,du =\frac{\lambda^r h^d}{d!}A^{-1}(B-I).$$

Every eigenvalue of $A$ has strictly negative real part, so $A$, $B$ and $B-I$ are invertible, including when $Q$ has zero eigenvalues or Jordan blocks. Consequently $F_d$ is invertible.

Multiply the block rows and columns of $\mathsf H_0$ by the appropriate powers of $B^{-1}$ to obtain $[F(i+j)]$. Forward-difference row and column operations turn this into $[\Delta^{i+j}F(0)]$. This block matrix has zeros when $i+j>d$ and the same invertible block $d!F_d$ on its antidiagonal, proving $\mathsf H_0$ invertible.

The sequence $H_k$ is annihilated by $(S-B)^r$, where $S$ shifts $k$ forward and $B$ multiplies on the left. Hence $\mathsf H_1\mathsf H_0^{-1}$ is a block companion matrix whose last diagonal block is $rB$. Finally $D=\Delta^dF(0)=d!F_d=\lambda^r h^d A^{-1}(B-I)$, which gives the displayed formula for $Q$.

This is a matrix-valued confluent-Prony argument: finite differences, recurrences and Hankel reconstruction are classical, as in Batenkov and Yomdin (2013), Section 2. The proof here specializes those elementary ideas to joint labeled endpoint/duration-bin matrices; no new general Prony principle or priority for alias-free sampling is claimed.

An exact discarded-time alias that the bins separate

Take shape $r=7$, rate $\lambda=1$, bin width $h=1$, $P=\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top/3$, $E=I-P$ and

$$J_0=\frac1{\sqrt3}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&-1\\-1&0&1\\1&-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \theta=\pi/7,\quad a=10\cos\theta-1,\quad b=10\sin\theta,\quad Q_\pm=-aE\pm bJ_0.$$

Both generators have strictly positive off-diagonal rates, with minimum $0.164867290576$. Because $J_0^2=-E$ and $PJ_0=J_0P=0$, their resolvents and discarded-time kernels satisfy

$$(I-Q_\pm)^{-1}=P+\tfrac1{10}\{\cos\theta E\pm\sin\theta J_0\},\qquad R_\pm=(I-Q_\pm)^{-7}=P-10^{-7}E.$$

Thus discarding durations gives exactly the same label-path laws, even though the full clock law is known. Retaining the 15-valued category separates the pair: the first-bin matrix infinity-norm gap is $6.614059059312\times10^{-8}$. These are time reversals; no difference in their symmetric Green–Kubo strengths or ranks is claimed.

Identification is not a numerical or statistical guarantee

For this alias example, the block Hankel infinity-condition number has base-ten logarithm $49.001$. The certificate uses 180-digit arithmetic for its recovery; ordinary binary64 inversion is not presented as reliable for this case. Finite invertibility alone supplies no uniform conditioning, finite-sample accuracy or calendar-time efficiency.

There is also an exact fixed-clock obstruction to uniform inverse continuity. For two-state reset generators $Q_c=c(P-I)$ with $P=\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top/2$ and a common uniform start, the endpoint/category laws satisfy

$$\|\mathcal L(Q_c)-\mathcal L(Q_{2c})\|_{\rm TV} =\frac12\left\{\left(\frac\lambda{\lambda+c}\right)^r- \left(\frac\lambda{\lambda+2c}\right)^r\right\}\longrightarrow0,\qquad \|Q_c-Q_{2c}\|_\infty=c\longrightarrow\infty.$$

Indeed $H_k(Q_c)=p_kP+v_k(c)(I-P)$, with $v_k(c)=\mathbb E[e^{-cT}\mathbf1_{\{J=k\}}]$ decreasing in $c$. Summing the nonnegative differences over all bins, including overflow, gives the Erlang Laplace-transform difference above. This does not preclude local error bounds when conditioning is controlled.

The Erlang-family assumption is essential when the duration is quantized. For example, with $h=1$ and two stages, let the intervals be iid uniform on $[1/8,1/6]$; double every interval and replace $Q$ by $Q/2$. All sampled label laws are unchanged, and the two totals lie in $[1/4,1/3]$ and $[1/2,2/3]$, both entirely in bin zero.

These are continuous-clock counterexamples to identification under an unrestricted unknown interval family, not counterexamples within the stipulated Erlang family. State-dependent sampling, unbounded unknown shape, mislabeled states and elapsed-time stopping are outside this finite-category result. The theorem changes neither fixed-feature Green–Kubo rank nor rank after integrating maturity-dependent loadings.

The certificate checks exact finite-difference identities, unknown bounded-shape/rate calibration, directed/defective/absorbing recoveries, independent bin integrals and the shape-seven alias. Tolerances are portable and its last line is literal PASS; the high-precision floating calculations are not outward-rounded interval certificates.
