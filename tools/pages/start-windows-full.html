Raw-window confidence budgets from any start

Stationary moment targets can be estimated from a trajectory started anywhere, with an explicit finite-record transient term. This does not make nonstationary one-block moments obey stationary inversion identities. The bound is model-dependent and holds for every starting state, without a burn-in period or knowing the stationary law.

Setup and theorem

Let $Y$ have a fixed finite irreducible row generator $Q$ on $n\ge2$ known labeled states, stationary law $\pi$, and an arbitrary initial law. Fix $T>0$, an integer $N\ge1$, $0<\delta<1$, and a certified Dobrushin upper bound $0\le\alpha<1$ for $P=e^{3TQ}$ before observing the record. Put

$$O_{kj}=\int_{(3k+j)T}^{(3k+j+1)T}e_{Y_t}dt,\qquad \widehat A_i=N^{-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}O_{k0}O_{k,i+1}^T,\quad i=0,1.$$

The targets $A_i=\mathbb E_\pi[O_{00}O_{0,i+1}^T]$ are the stationary raw ordered moments. The data need not start in stationarity, and no fitted mean is subtracted.

$$c_0=0,\quad c_{j+1}=1+\alpha c_j,\quad H_N^\circ=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(1+c_j)^2,$$ $$\eta_{\rm samp}=\frac{nT^2}{N}\sqrt{\frac{H_N^\circ}{2}\log\frac{4n^2}{\delta}},\qquad \eta_{\rm start}=\frac{\sqrt2\,T^2c_N}{N}.$$
$$\boxed{\inf_x\Pr_x\!\left(\|\widehat A_i-A_i\|_F\le \eta_{\rm samp}+\eta_{\rm start}\text{ for both }i=0,1\right)\ge1-\delta.}$$

Here $\Pr_x$ means the law conditional on $Y_0=x$ under the specified Markov model. Integrating this bound covers every unknown, mixed or degenerate initial law. For fixed design and confidence level the sampling term is $O(N^{-1/2})$ and the transient term is $O(N^{-1})$; the latter has no dimension factor.

Fluctuations conditional on the initial state

For one product entry let $Z_k\in[0,T^2]$ be its reward in block $k$, let $g(x)=\mathbb E_xZ_0$, and write $V_j=\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-1}P^\ell g$. Oscillation contraction gives $\operatorname{osc}(V_j)\le T^2c_j$.

Condition on $Y_0=x$, then reveal successive complete path blocks. Each Doob martingale increment for $\sum_kZ_k$ has conditional range at most $T^2(1+c_{N-k-1})$, retaining reward–endpoint dependence. There is no random-initial-state increment under this conditioning.

The conditional Hoeffding lemma gives

$$\Pr_x\!\left(\left|N^{-1}\sum_kZ_k-\mathbb E_x[N^{-1}\sum_kZ_k]\right|\ge t\right) \le2\exp\{-2N^2t^2/(T^4H_N^\circ)\}.$$

A union bound over both matrices and their $n^2$ entries gives the Frobenius sampling radius $\eta_{\rm samp}$ around the conditional mean, uniformly in $x$. This step does not replace that conditional mean by the stationary target.

A dimension-free transient term

Let $K_i(x)=\mathbb E_x[O_{00}O_{0,i+1}^T]$. Every $K_i(x)/T^2$ is a probability vector on the $n^2$ ordered state pairs: its entries are nonnegative and sum to one. Consequently the map from a block's starting law to its expected matrix is a Markov kernel.

Total-variation contraction and the simplex inequality $\|p-q\|_2\le\sqrt2\,d_{\rm TV}(p,q)$ imply

$$\left\|\mathbb E_x\widehat A_i-A_i\right\|_F \le\frac{\sqrt2T^2}{N}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}d_{\rm TV}(e_xP^k,\pi) \le\frac{\sqrt2T^2c_N}{N}.$$

Here $d_{\rm TV}(e_xP^k,\pi)\le\alpha^k$, including $k=0$. Adding this deterministic mean bias to the conditional sampling bound proves the theorem.

Exact bias coefficient and sharp order

Flatten each $K_i(x)$ into a row, write $\Pi=\mathbf1\pi$ and $H=(I-P+\Pi)^{-1}-\Pi$. The finite geometric identity gives, for any starting prior $\nu$,

$$\mathbb E_\nu\widehat A_i-A_i =\frac1N\nu(I-P^N)HK_i,\qquad N(\mathbb E_\nu\widehat A_i-A_i)\longrightarrow\nu HK_i.$$

These formulas describe the bias when a model is specified; the confidence budget does not require computing its unknown coefficient. They are identities for flattened matrices, with the result reshaped back to $n\times n$.

For the symmetric two-state chain flipping at rate $1/2$, take $T=\log2$ and start in state one. Since $\widehat\pi^T=T^{-2}\widehat A_0\mathbf1$,

$$\mathbb E_1\widehat\pi-\pi =\frac{2(1-8^{-N})}{7N\log2}(1,-1).$$

Each component's nonzero limiting magnitude is $2/(7\log2)\approx0.412198583$. Since taking row sums is a bounded linear map, this also proves that the full raw-moment transient bias is generally not $o(N^{-1})$.

Reconstruction and grid observations

The supplied budgets can feed the stationary-target raw-moment inverse and its residual-aware error certificate. Stationary population targets are unchanged; the new term accounts for estimating them from a transient record. A candidate may be selected from the same data, but failed sufficient tests still require abstention.

For aligned uniform left-endpoint grid occupations of spacing $h$, $T/h$ an integer, the same proof applies to their stationary grid targets: the block rewards remain nonnegative with total mass $T^2$. With a separately justified exit-rate bound $a\ge\max_x(-Q_{xx})$, the stationary population mesh bound then gives budgets for the exact continuous targets

$$\eta_i=\eta_{\rm samp}+\eta_{\rm start}+T^2h^2a^2/3,\qquad i=0,1.$$

This decomposition does not assume that a nonstationary one-block quadrature error has the stationary quadratic cancellation.

Checks, classical tools and scope

The certificate checks $648$ conditional ranges and $72$ squared transient-bias inequalities exactly in a dependent three-state block model. It also computes $12$ exact conditional finite laws, cross-checks their moment-generating functions, and verifies $612$ continuous-time starting-state/channel cases on $13$ chains, including sparse and defective examples. Floating cross-checks are not interval proofs or empirical evidence of the confidence theorem.

Markov-chain concentration, initial-state effects and additive Poisson martingale methods are classical; see Glynn and Ormoneit (2002), Theorem 2 and Section 2. This note specializes them to raw path-window matrices and separates conditional fluctuations from their dimension-free stationary-target bias.

The guarantee is model-dependent and conditional on the initial state, not distribution-free feature-conditional conformal coverage. Even conditional on that state, the probability of reporting an incorrect certified reconstruction is at most $\delta$; coverage conditional on reporting is not asserted. Known labels, fixed record design and independently justified mixing remain essential; adaptive stopping, hidden states, noisy occupations and arithmetic error need separate control.
