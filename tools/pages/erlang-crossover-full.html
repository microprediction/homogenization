Three short bond quotes can alias an Erlang-stage prior

Near equal stage rates, a precise cancellation prevents a uniform short-maturity identification threshold.

Result and quantifiers

Consider two alternating regimes with two exponential stages each, stage rates $x,y>0$, known Vasicek parameters $\kappa>0$, $\theta_1\ne\theta_2$, common $\sigma\ge0$ and known current short rate $r$. The unknown is a prior over all four stages, including uncertainty about the current regime. Fix any three ordered nodes $0<t_1<t_2<t_3$ and put $d=(\theta_1-\theta_2)/2\ne0$.

For every sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$, there is a positive rate

$$y_\varepsilon=x+\frac45d\kappa(t_1+t_2+t_3)\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon^2)$$

such that two distinct, strictly positive stage priors give exactly the same three bond prices at maturities $\varepsilon t_1,\varepsilon t_2,\varepsilon t_3$, under the same known parameters $(x,y_\varepsilon)$. Their full bond curves are nevertheless different. The rate depends on the design scale: this does not contradict identification at all sufficiently small maturities for one fixed model.

Normalized prices and exact jets

Let the stage generator and level-sign matrix be

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}-x&x&0&0\\0&-x&x&0\\0&0&-y&y\\y&0&0&-y\end{pmatrix},\qquad D=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1).$$

The short rate satisfies $dr_t=\kappa(\theta_{Y_t}-r_t)dt+\sigma dW_t$, with Brownian motion independent of the stage chain. With $B(T)=(1-e^{-\kappa T})/\kappa$, the exact linear amplitude solves $P_j=a_j e^{-Br}$, $a'=[Q+\operatorname{diag}(-\kappa\theta B+\sigma^2B^2/2)]a$, $a(0)=\mathbf1$.

Remove the known common factor $f(T)=\exp\int_0^T[-\kappa\theta_*B(s)+\sigma^2B(s)^2/2]ds$, with midpoint $\theta_*=(\theta_1+\theta_2)/2$, not the stationary mean. Then $z=a/f$ satisfies $z'=[Q-d(1-e^{-\kappa T})D]z$, and the normalized mixture quote is $e^{Br}P_\nu/f=\nu z$. Write $z(T)=\sum_{n\ge0}u_nT^n$; its exact recursion is

$$(n+1)u_{n+1}=Qu_n+dD\sum_{h=1}^n\frac{(-\kappa)^h}{h!}u_{n-h},\qquad u_0=\mathbf1,\quad u_1=0.$$

For $q=4,5$, let $C_q$ have rows $u_0^\top,u_2^\top,u_3^\top,u_q^\top$. Substituting the recursion yields

$$\det C_4=\frac{d^3\kappa^3xy(x-y)}{18},\qquad \det C_5\big|_{y=x}=\frac{2d^4\kappa^4x^2}{45}.$$

The cancellation and an exact singular branch

Eliminate the fourth weight using normalization, giving the three-by-three response $H_{j\ell}(\varepsilon,y)=z_\ell(\varepsilon t_j)-z_4(\varepsilon t_j)$. Let $V_q=\det[t_j^p]_{j=1,2,3;\ p=2,3,q}$; then

$$\det H=-\det C_4\,V_4\varepsilon^9-\det C_5\,V_5\varepsilon^{10}+O(\varepsilon^{11}),\qquad V_4=\prod_jt_j^2\prod_{a<b}(t_b-t_a)>0,\quad V_5=(t_1+t_2+t_3)V_4.$$

The remainder is locally uniform for $y$ near $x$ because the ODE solution is analytic in maturity and rates. Substitute $y=x+\varepsilon\eta$ and divide by $A\varepsilon^{10}$, where $A=d^3\kappa^3x^2V_4/18\ne0$. The resulting function extends analytically through $\varepsilon=0$ and has

$$F(\varepsilon,\eta):=\frac{\det H(\varepsilon,x+\varepsilon\eta)}{A\varepsilon^{10}},\qquad F(0,\eta)=\eta-\frac45d\kappa(t_1+t_2+t_3).$$

At $\eta_*=(4/5)d\kappa(t_1+t_2+t_3)$, $F(0,\eta_*)=0$ and $\partial_\eta F(0,\eta_*)=1$. The analytic implicit-function theorem therefore supplies a unique local branch $\eta(\varepsilon)=\eta_*+O(\varepsilon)$ with $F(\varepsilon,\eta(\varepsilon))=0$. Taking $y_\varepsilon=x+\varepsilon\eta(\varepsilon)$ proves the asserted rate expansion and positivity for small $\varepsilon$.

Rank two gives genuine positive-prior aliases

The minor in the first two quote rows and first two response columns has leading term

$$-\frac{d^2\kappa^2x}{3}\,t_1^2t_2^2(t_2-t_1)\varepsilon^5,$$

uniformly for bounded $\eta$. It is nonzero for small positive $\varepsilon$, so the singular response on the branch has rank exactly two, not zero. For any nonzero $v\in\ker H$, put $w=(v_1,v_2,v_3,-\sum v_\ell)$ and normalize $\|w\|_\infty=1$.

The priors $\nu^\pm=\mathbf1/4\pm w/8$ have every component at least $1/8$, sum to one and are distinct. Their price differences at the three maturities vanish exactly, since $H(w_1,w_2,w_3)^\top=0$ after eliminating the fourth coordinate.

For every sufficiently small positive branch value, $y_\varepsilon\ne x$ because $\eta_*\ne0$. Therefore $\det C_4\ne0$: the augmented jets distinguish all four weights, and the full curves cannot coincide for distinct priors. Other sufficiently short quote designs for this fixed model do identify them.

Scope, context and checks

There is no common positive maturity threshold valid for every model in any neighborhood of equal rates, even with all parameters known and the stage rates confined to a compact subset of $(0,\infty)$. This is an exact finite-design alias, stronger than mere bad conditioning, but not nonidentification from the entire curve. It concerns initial-prior observability, not identification of parameters or a regime count from maturity-integrated Green–Kubo rank.

The observability framework is classical; Kalman (1960), Section 5, defines observability and its Gramian. The model-specific jet cancellation and implicit branch are proved here; no new general observability principle is claimed.

For $x=2$, $d=\kappa=1$ and nodes $(1,2,3)$, the limiting gap coefficient is $\eta_*=24/5$. At $\varepsilon=1/1024$, the numerical branch estimate is $\eta=4.798382900402$, $y=2.004685920801$; independent ODE amplitudes agree with the Taylor evaluation within $3.43\times10^{-64}$. Rounded rates do not themselves define an exact alias: the analytic root above does.

The certificate proves the limiting and rank-minor identities symbolically, checks four branch approximations and constructs positive numerical prior pairs. Its final line is literal PASS and its assertions use portable tolerances; high-precision roots and ODE checks are not outward-rounded interval certificates.
