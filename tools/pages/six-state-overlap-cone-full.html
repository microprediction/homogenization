All six-state two-cycle Green–Kubo cones

A sharp repeated-mode bound for flows generated by at most two simple directed cycles.

Research note, 21 October 2026. This is a restricted architecture theorem, not a solution of the unrestricted minimum-state problem.

Result

Let $c_A,c_B$ be any two simple directed cycles whose union connects all six states, let $P_A,P_B$ be their edge-incidence matrices, and set $W=aP_A+bP_B$, where $a,b>0$. If $\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge0$ are the two intrinsic energy-normalized skew frequencies, then

$$\max_{\substack{c_A,c_B\\\text{connected union}}}\max_{a,b>0}\sigma_2(a,b)=\sqrt{\frac35}.$$

The bound is attained at equal weights by, for example, the cycles

$$c_A=(0,5,2,3,4,1),\qquad c_B=(0,3,4,5,2,1),$$

whose union is neither a cactus nor translation invariant. Within this cone equality holds exactly when $a=b>0$, and then both frequencies equal $\sqrt{3/5}$.

A one-cycle irreducible flow must itself be Hamiltonian and satisfies the same bound. Consequently no four-feature compression from any stationary flow with a decomposition into at most two cycles realizes $I_4+\cot(\pi/5)\operatorname{diag}(J,J)$, where $J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The target needs two frequencies $\cot(\pi/5)=\sqrt{1+2/\sqrt5}>\sqrt{3/5}$.

Stationary-flow coordinates

Any prescribed positive stationary law $\pi$ gives a generator through $q_{ij}=W_{ij}/\pi_i$ for $i\ne j$. Thus varying holding times or $\pi$ changes the generator but not the stationary-flow Laplacian

$$C=\operatorname{diag}(W{\bf1})-W.$$

Delete one row and column and write the grounded form as $\bar C=H+D$, with $H=(\bar C+\bar C^\top)/2$ and $D=(\bar C-\bar C^\top)/2$. Irreducibility makes $H$ positive definite.

The eigenvalues of $H^{-1/2}DH^{-1/2}$ are $\{\pm i\sigma_1,\pm i\sigma_2,0\}$. Hence

$$\frac{\det(tH-D)}{\det H}=t(t^2+\sigma_1^2)(t^2+\sigma_2^2).$$

Exact pencil calculation

Direct expansion of the five-dimensional determinant gives

$$\frac{\det(tH-D)}{\det H}=t\{t^4+S(a,b)t^2+P(a,b)\},$$ $$S(a,b)=\frac{2(5a^4+12a^3b+11a^2b^2+12ab^3+5b^4)}{3(a^2+3ab+b^2)^2},$$ $$P(a,b)=\left(\frac{a^2+ab+b^2}{a^2+3ab+b^2}\right)^2.$$

Put $x=ab/(a+b)^2\in[0,1/4]$. Then

$$S(x)=\frac{2(5-8x-3x^2)}{3(1+x)^2},\qquad P(x)=\left(\frac{1-x}{1+x}\right)^2.$$

The squared frequencies are the roots of $f_x(z)=z^2-S(x)z+P(x)$. At the proposed sharp value,

$$f_x(3/5)=\frac{16(4x-1)}{25(1+x)}\le0.$$

Since $f_x$ is monic with two nonnegative real roots, $3/5$ lies between them. Therefore $\sigma_2^2\le3/5$.

Equality requires $x=1/4$, equivalently $a=b>0$. Substitution gives $t(t^2+3/5)^2$, proving both equality and multiplicity without a floating search.

Why the bound covers every pair

There are 409 simple directed cycles on six labeled states, counted up to cyclic rotation. Relabel the first cycle canonically according to its length, from two through six; retaining connected unions leaves 1,372 relative pairs and 133 distinct exact coefficient triples.

For each pencil write $r=b/a=s/(1-s)$ with $0\le s\le1$, and let $f(z)=z^2-Sz+P$ be its squared-frequency polynomial. Exact rational arithmetic covers $[0,1]$ by at most six dyadic intervals, on each of which Bernstein coefficients certify either

$$S\le\frac65\qquad\text{or}\qquad f(3/5)\le0.$$

The first inequality gives $\sigma_2^2\le S/2\le3/5$. In the second case $3/5$ lies between the two nonnegative roots, so again $\sigma_2^2\le3/5$.

Why feature choice cannot evade the bound

Let $U$ collect any four independent Poisson potentials and normalize their symmetric energy. The resulting feature skew matrix is the compression

$$X^\top(iH^{-1/2}DH^{-1/2})X,\qquad X^\top X=I_4.$$

Hermitian compression interlacing, in the form proved by Fan and Pall (1957), bounds its second positive eigenvalue by $\sigma_2$. This is a necessary condition for a fixed-parameter Green–Kubo block; it is not a rank claim after integrating different maturity loadings.

The target has symmetric part $I_4$ and two positive skew frequencies equal to $\cot(\pi/5)$. The strict comparison above therefore excludes every generator whose stationary flow is a nonnegative mixture of at most two simple cycles, for every positive stationary law and every four-feature embedding.

Certificate and scope

The certificate enumerates all 409 simple cycles and 1,372 canonical connected pairs, constructs all 133 exact coefficient triples, and proves the interval alternatives above with rational Bernstein coefficients. It separately verifies the displayed extremal formula and checks 300 independently chosen rates and stationary laws against the full whitened skew spectrum.

The portable assertion ceilings are $2\times10^{-11}$ for the spectrum formula and $2\times10^{-10}$ for stationarity. These floating checks test the implementation; the architecture-wide inequality is the analytic determinant argument above.

This result does not prove that $\sqrt{3/5}$ is the largest repeated frequency among all six-state balanced flows. Flows whose minimum cycle-decomposition size is three or more, and unrestricted state counts six through nine, remain open.
