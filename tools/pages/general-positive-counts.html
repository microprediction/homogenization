A positive count corrector for any finite regime chain

Exact moment matching and an all-prior, second-order total-variation bound.

Let a finite irreducible chain have generator $mQ$ and nonnegative arrival intensities $\ell$, not all zero. At a fixed horizon $T>0$, its Cox count is Poisson conditional on $\Lambda_T=\int_0^T\ell(Y_t)\,dt$.

For each initial prior $\nu$, fit a gamma variable to the exact mean $\mu_\nu$ and variance $v_\nu$ of $\Lambda_T$. Its Poisson mixture is a genuine negative-binomial law with generating function

$$\mathbb Ez^{N_{*,\nu}}= \left(1+\frac{v_\nu}{\mu_\nu}(1-z)\right)^{-\mu_\nu^2/v_\nu}.$$

The count mean $\mu_\nu$ and variance $\mu_\nu+v_\nu$ match exactly. If $v_\nu=0$, use the exact Poisson law instead.

$$\sup_\nu d_{\rm TV}\big(\mathcal L_\nu(N_T),\mathcal L(N_{*,\nu})\big) \leq \min\{1,B(m,T)\}=O(m^{-2}).$$

The full note gives $B$ explicitly from a computable semigroup mixing envelope. No reversibility, diagonalizability, symmetric rates or stationary start is required; zero-intensity regimes are allowed.

The certificate checks 420 moment cases and 100 count laws, including a defective generator. For a directed cycle, a pure start and $T=1$, the error at speed 320 is $0.0000018292$, below the all-prior bound $0.0162087908$.

The rate holds for fixed $Q,\ell,T$, uniformly over initial priors, including speed-dependent ones. It does not give a bound uniform over closing spectral gaps, long maturities or the full arrival process.
