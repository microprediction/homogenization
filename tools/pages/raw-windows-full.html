Recovering the stationary law as well as the generator

With complete labeled state occupations, two raw ordered window-moment matrices identify both the stationary law and the generator. A residual-aware error bound propagates uncertainty in the stationary law instead of treating its estimate as exact. Raw moments retain mean information discarded by centered covariances; this does not recover an unknown law from centered covariances alone.

Exact population inversion

Let $Y$ be a stationary finite irreducible chain with row generator $Q$ on a known labeled state space of size $n\ge2$. Fix a time unit and $T>0$, and write $O_j=\int_{jT}^{(j+1)T}e_{Y_t}dt$ and $A_i=\mathbb E[O_0O_{i+1}^T]$, $i=0,1$. These are raw moments, not centered covariances.

Set $D=\operatorname{diag}\pi$, $\Pi=\mathbf1\pi$, $B=\int_0^Te^{sQ}ds$, and choose any fixed $\lambda>0$ with units of inverse time. Integrating the ordered transition kernel gives $A_0=DB^2$ and $A_1=A_0e^{TQ}$, hence

$$\pi^T=T^{-2}A_0\mathbf1,\quad E=A_0^{-1}A_1,\quad Z=(I-E)^2A_0^{-1}D+\lambda^2\Pi=Q^2+\lambda^2\Pi,$$
$$\boxed{Q=\lambda\Pi-Z^{1/2}_{\rm principal}.}$$

The matrix $B$ is invertible: its eigenvalue at the stationary mode is $T$, and at any nonzero eigenvalue $z$ of $Q$ it is $(e^{Tz}-1)/z\ne0$. Since $Q\Pi=\Pi Q=0$, $(-Q+\lambda\Pi)^2=Z$, and $-Q+\lambda\Pi$ has spectrum strictly in the open right half-plane. The principal root is therefore $-Q+\lambda\Pi$, including for defective generators.

Deterministic certificate with stationary-law error

Suppose $\|\widehat A_i-A_i\|_F\le\eta_i$ are supplied valid error budgets. Require $\widehat A_0$ invertible and $\widehat\pi^T=T^{-2}\widehat A_0\mathbf1$ a strictly positive probability vector; otherwise abstain. Put $\widehat D=\operatorname{diag}\widehat\pi$, $\widehat\Pi=\mathbf1\widehat\pi$, and

$$\widehat C=\widehat A_0^{-1},\quad\widehat E=\widehat C\widehat A_1,\quad \widehat F=I-\widehat E,\quad \widehat Z=\widehat F^2\widehat C\widehat D+\lambda^2\widehat\Pi.$$

All of the following bounds use observable quantities and the supplied budgets:

$$k=\|\widehat C\|_2,\quad q=k\eta_0<1,\quad a=k/(1-q),\quad b=ak\eta_0,$$ $$d=a(\eta_1+\eta_0\|\widehat E\|_2),\quad f=\|\widehat F\|_2,\quad x=\sqrt n\eta_0/T^2,\quad p_-=\min_i\widehat\pi_i,\quad p_+=\min(1,\max_i\widehat\pi_i+x),$$ $$\Gamma=p_+\{a(2fd+d^2)+f^2b\}+(f^2k+\lambda^2\sqrt n)x.$$

Choose any real candidate $R$ for the square root in the $\widehat D$ coordinates. Supply certified bounds $\operatorname{sym}R\succeq\mu I$, $\mu>0$, and $\zeta\ge\|R^2-\widehat D^{1/2}\widehat Z\widehat D^{-1/2}\|_F$.

$$\Delta=\sqrt{\max_i\widehat\pi_i/p_-}\,\Gamma+\zeta<\mu^2,\quad r=\frac{\Delta}{\mu+\sqrt{\mu^2-\Delta}},\quad \overline Q=\lambda\widehat\Pi-\widehat D^{-1/2}R\widehat D^{1/2}.$$
$$\boxed{\|\widehat\pi-\pi\|_2\le x,\qquad \|\widehat D^{1/2}(\overline Q-Q)\widehat D^{-1/2}\|_F \le r+\lambda x/\sqrt{p_-}.}$$

The generator norm uses the observed law $\widehat\pi$, not the unknown $\pi$. Each entry has the corresponding bound multiplied by $\sqrt{\widehat\pi_j/\widehat\pi_i}$. A passing error radius need not certify nonnegative rates, zero row sums, or $\widehat\pi\overline Q=0$; failed tests are inconclusive.

Proof of the certificate

Write $C=A_0^{-1}$ and $F=I-E$. The Neumann bound and $E-\widehat E=C[(A_1-\widehat A_1)-(A_0-\widehat A_0)\widehat E]$ give $\|C\|_2\le a$, $\|C-\widehat C\|_F\le b$, $\|E-\widehat E\|_F\le d$, and $\|F^2-\widehat F^2\|_F\le2fd+d^2$.

The row-sum identity gives $\|\widehat\pi-\pi\|_2\le x$, hence $\|D\|_2\le p_+$, $\|D-\widehat D\|_F\le x$ and $\|\Pi-\widehat\Pi\|_F\le\sqrt n x$. Expanding $F^2CD-\widehat F^2\widehat C\widehat D$ and the stationary-projector term proves $\|Z-\widehat Z\|_F\le\Gamma$.

Let $W=\widehat D^{1/2}(-Q+\lambda\Pi)\widehat D^{-1/2}$. Similarity and the residual yield $\|W^2-R^2\|_F\le\Delta$; the Sylvester inverse $Y\mapsto\int_0^\infty e^{-tR}Ye^{-tR}dt$ has Frobenius operator norm at most $1/(2\mu)$.

For $D_0=W^2-R^2$, the map $X\mapsto\mathcal S^{-1}(D_0-X^2)$ contracts the radius-$r$ Frobenius ball, since $\Delta+r^2=2\mu r$ and $r<\mu$. Its fixed point has $R+X$ strictly accretive and $(R+X)^2=W^2$; principal-root uniqueness gives $R+X=W$ even though $W$ need not be accretive in the estimated-law metric.

Finally, $\|\widehat D^{1/2}(\widehat\Pi-\Pi)\widehat D^{-1/2}\|_F =\{\sum_i(\widehat\pi_i-\pi_i)^2/\widehat\pi_i\}^{1/2}\le x/\sqrt{p_-}$. The triangle inequality separates this projector error from $\|R-W\|_F\le r$, proving the claim.

Finite-sample use without knowing the stationary law

For one stationary path over $[0,3NT]$, average the raw products $O_{k0}O_{k,i+1}^T$, where $O_{kj}=\int_{(3k+j)T}^{(3k+j+1)T}e_{Y_t}dt$. Their entries lie in $[0,T^2]$; their total mass is $T^2$, so the row-sum estimate is automatically a probability vector, possibly with zero entries.

Given a fixed certified Dobrushin bound $0\le\alpha<1$ for $e^{3TQ}$, set $c_0=0$, $c_{m+1}=1+\alpha c_m$, and $H_N=c_N^2+\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}(1+c_m)^2$. Simultaneous valid budgets at confidence $1-\delta$ are

$$\eta_0=\eta_1=\frac{nT^2}{N}\sqrt{\frac{H_N}{2}\log\frac{4n^2}{\delta}}.$$

Indeed, for each entry the expected future reward has oscillation at most $T^2c_m$. Revealing the initial state and successive whole blocks gives conditional martingale ranges $T^2c_N$ and $T^2(1+c_m)$, so conditional Hoeffding bounds and a union bound give the formula. No centering by a known $\pi$ is used; the one-sided range also improves the generic signed-reward bound by a factor of two.

On this simultaneous event every passing reconstruction candidate has its error bound, even when selected using the same data. Thus the probability of reporting an incorrect certified radius is at most $\delta$, not a coverage guarantee conditional on reporting. The design and mixing bound must have independent justification; they cannot be selected from the fitted generator without additional control.

Checks and limits

The certificate verifies $57$ exact-input reconstructions on $19$ chains, including nonuniform, sparse and defective examples, and $342$ perturbed-input bounds with nonzero stationary-law errors and root residuals. An exact rational perturbation attains the squared row-sum error factor $n$, and $96$ exact nonnegative-reward range checks support the confidence-budget constants. Continuous-time input moments are computed independently with Feynman–Kac Fréchet derivatives.

Known state labels, complete state occupations, stationary starts and the time unit remain essential. This is not hidden-state recovery, a uniform conditioning result as stationary masses vanish, or a guarantee for discretized/noisy occupations. Rigorous numerical use must enclose all arithmetic errors; the floating-point checks are not interval proofs.

Principal-root uniqueness and conditioning are classical; see Higham (2008), Chapter 6. The concentration proof uses classical conditional exponential bounds as in Glynn and Ormoneit (2002), Section 2. The addition here is the raw-moment inversion and explicit propagation of stationary-law uncertainty.
