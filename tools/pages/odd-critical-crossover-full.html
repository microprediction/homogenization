Odd critical scores at every noise scale

An exact telegraph contrast, a uniform crossover, and finite-threshold error budgets.

Flattening an increasing score does not change its noiseless quantile event. For odd powers of a two-state occupation average, start-dependent population coverage remains of order $c^{-1/2}$ without noise, although fixed positive Gaussian noise reduces it to order $c^{-(d+1)/2}$.

Setup and result

Let $Y_t\in\{-1,+1\}$ flip at rate one, $A_c=c^{-1}\int_0^cY_s\,ds$, and $W_c=\sqrt c A_c$. Fix an odd integer $d\ge3$, $\mu>0$ and $p\in(0,1)$, and use the score $S_c=\mu A_c^d+\sigma Z$, where $Z$ is independent standard Gaussian and $\sigma\ge0$.

Write $q_c$ for its exact stationary pooled $p$-quantile and $g_+(c,\sigma)=\Pr_+(S_c\le q_c)-p$. At zero noise take $c$ sufficiently large that $p$ lies between the endpoint atom levels. These are population thresholds, not thresholds estimated from a calibration sample.

$$\lambda=c^{d/2}\sigma/\mu,\quad r_d(t)=\operatorname{sgn}(t)|t|^{1/d},\quad P_d(t)=-\tfrac12\phi(r_d(t)),\quad K_{d,\lambda}(t)=\mathbb E P_d(t-\lambda Z).$$

Let $t_{p,d}(\lambda)$ be the $p$-quantile of $G^d+\lambda Z$ for independent standard Gaussians $G,Z$. For each fixed $d,p$, the following is uniform over every noise level:

$$g_+(c,\sigma)=c^{-1/2}K_{d,\lambda}(t_{p,d}(\lambda))+R_{c,d,p}(\lambda),\qquad \sup_{\lambda\ge0}\sqrt c(1+\lambda)|R_{c,d,p}(\lambda)|\longrightarrow0.$$

The negative-start gap is exactly $-g_+$, and $g_+<0$ at every finite threshold with positive noise. Thus marginal population coverage is exact, but coverage conditional on the latent start need not be. This supplies neither finite-calibration nor distribution-free or training-conditional coverage.

$$\sqrt c\,g_+(c,0)\longrightarrow-\tfrac12\phi(z_p),\qquad c^{(d+1)/2}\sigma_c g_+(c,\sigma_c)\longrightarrow-\tfrac12\mu d!!\phi(z_p) \quad\text{if }\lambda_c\longrightarrow\infty,$$

Here $z_p=\Phi^{-1}(p)$ and $d!!=d(d-2)\cdots1$. The second limit includes fixed positive noise; it does not describe noise below the crossover scale $c^{-d/2}$. The first limit is independent of the odd degree because the noiseless quantile event is exactly preserved.

Exact signed CDF and finite bounds

For $-1\le a<1$, the right-continuous occupation CDFs satisfy

$$F_+(a)-F_\pi(a)=-\tfrac12e^{-c}I_0(c\sqrt{1-a^2}).$$

The difference is zero outside that half-open interval, including at $a=1$. To prove the identity, condition on the Poisson number of flips, as in the path-dependent note.

Odd flip counts have identical start-conditional laws. At $2j\ge2$ flips, the plus-start minus pooled CDF of $U=(1+A_c)/2$ is $-[u(1-u)]^j/[j B(j,j)]$.

Multiplying by $e^{-c}c^{2j}/(2j)!$ and adding the no-flip term gives $-e^{-c}\sum_{j\ge0}[c^2u(1-u)]^j/(2(j!)^2)$, the displayed Bessel expression. Its negativity persists under every increasing odd-power transformation and convolution with independent Gaussian noise.

Set $D_c(x)=\sqrt c[F_+(x/\sqrt c)-F_\pi(x/\sqrt c)]$. For $c\ge2$, put $m_j=\mathbb E|G|^j$ and define

$$U_c=\frac3c+(\sqrt c+1)e^{-c/4},$$ $$L_{d,c}=\frac d{2c}(6m_{d-1}+m_{d+1}+m_{d+3})+ \frac d2(\sqrt{2\pi c}+1)\mathbb E[|G|^{d-1}\mathbf1_{\{|G|>\sqrt{c/2}\}}].$$

Then $\|D_c+\phi/2\|_\infty\le U_c$ and $\|D_c\circ r_d-P_d\|_1\le L_{d,c}$, and both budgets are $O_d(c^{-1})$. Consequently, at any actual scaled threshold $t=c^{d/2}q/\mu$,

$$\left|\Pr_+(S_c\le q)-\Pr_\pi(S_c\le q)-c^{-1/2}K_{d,\lambda}(t)\right| \le c^{-1/2}\min\left\{U_c,\frac{L_{d,c}}{\lambda\sqrt{2\pi}}\right\}.$$

Omit the second budget at $\lambda=0$. This explicit finite-$c$ bound uses the actual threshold, not $t_{p,d}(\lambda)$. Replacing it by the reference quantile gives the uniform little-$o$ theorem above, not an additional explicit finite-quantile bound.

Proof of the budgets

The classical NIST integral representation gives $|\sqrt{2\pi z}e^{-z}I_0(z)-1|\le2/z$ for $z\ge1$, as follows directly.

After $s=\sqrt z\theta$, compare $e^{-z(1-\cos(s/\sqrt z))}$ with $e^{-s^2/2}$ on $[0,\pi\sqrt z]$. The inequalities $z(1-\cos(s/\sqrt z))\ge2s^2/\pi^2$ and $0\le s^2/2-z(1-\cos(s/\sqrt z))\le s^4/(24z)$ bound their normalized integral difference by $\pi^5/(256z)$.

The missing normalized Gaussian tail is at most $\sqrt{2/\pi}/(\pi z)$. The sum of these constants is less than two, proving the stated bound without an asymptotic remainder assumption.

For $x^2\le c/2$, put $v=x^2/c$ and $z=c\sqrt{1-v}$. The factors $(1-v)^{-1/4}\le2$, $|(1-v)^{-1/4}-1|\le v$, and $0\le c-z-x^2/2\le x^4/(2c)$ give

$$|D_c(x)+\phi(x)/2|\le\frac{\phi(x)}{2c}(6+x^2+x^4).$$

Indeed, write the factor relative to $-\phi(x)/2$ as $FB e^{-\delta}$ and use $(FB-1)e^{-\delta}+(e^{-\delta}-1)$, with $F=(1-v)^{-1/4}$ and $|B-1|\le3/c$. Outside this central region, $I_0(z)\le e^z$ and $c-z\ge x^2/2$ yield $|D_c(x)+\phi(x)/2|\le(\sqrt c/2+1/(2\sqrt{2\pi}))e^{-x^2/2}$, also beyond the support.

Taking suprema proves $U_c$; integrating with $dt=d|x|^{d-1}dx$ proves $L_{d,c}$. Gaussian convolution is an $L^\infty$ contraction and has density bounded by $1/(\lambda\sqrt{2\pi})$, proving the finite-threshold inequality.

Uniform quantile transfer

The stationary characteristic function is $e^{-c}[\cosh(c\omega)+\sinh(c\omega)/\omega]$, $\omega=\sqrt{1-t^2/c}$, so $W_c\Rightarrow G$. Compactify noise by $\rho=\lambda/(1+\lambda)$: the laws of $(1-\rho)W_c^d+\rho Z$ converge along every convergent sequence of $\rho$.

Every limiting CDF is continuous and strictly increasing, including the endpoints $\rho=0,1$. Hence its fixed interior $p$-quantile is continuous in $\rho$, and the exact normalized quantiles converge uniformly on $[0,1]$.

The weighted profile $H_\rho(u)=(1+\lambda)K_{d,\lambda}((1+\lambda)u)$ is jointly continuous on compact sets. At $\rho=0$ this follows from uniform continuity of $P_d$; for $\rho>0$ use

$$H_\rho(u)=\rho^{-1}\int_{\mathbb R}P_d(v) \phi((u-(1-\rho)v)/\rho)\,dv.$$

Since $P_d\in L^1$ and $\int P_d=-d!!/2$, this extends at $\rho=1$ to $-d!!\phi(u)/2$. Uniform quantile transfer and the finite-threshold budget, multiplied by $\sqrt c(1+\lambda)$, now prove the theorem and both endpoint limits.

Certificate and scope

The certificate checks the independent Poisson–Beta CDF series against the Bessel contrast, verifies the budgets, and tests degrees $3,5,7$ at $c=16,64,256,1024$ across vanishing, crossover, fixed and increasing noise. For $p=0.9$, degree three and $c=1024$, its large-noise weighted contrast is $-0.2629904281$ against the limit $-0.2632474979$.

These deterministic quadrature checks support the analytic proof; the finite grid is not a proof of uniformity. The telegraph distribution and Gaussian limit are classical: see Bogachev and Ratanov (2011), equation (1.9) and Theorem 1.1.

The present result concerns the velocity occupation average, not occupation of the positive half-line by the integrated telegraph position. Arbitrary finite-state drivers and general scores with an odd leading critical term require separate error control.
