Two large skew modes cannot fit in a small cyclic chain

A Fourier and compression exclusion for every translation-invariant chain on five through eight cyclic states.

A cyclically translation-invariant chain with $5\le n\le8$ has at most one intrinsic Green–Kubo skew frequency larger than one. Consequently no four-feature compression can have two frequencies equal to $\cot(\pi/5)$.

The chain and its full intrinsic frequencies

Write the state space as $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ and let

$$Qf(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}r_j\{f(x+j)-f(x)\},\qquad r_j\ge0.$$

Assume that the jumps with positive rates generate the group, so the chain is irreducible and its stationary law is uniform. The support may be dense and noncactus; nearest-neighbour or reversible symmetry is not assumed.

On centered real potentials put

$$L=-Q,\qquad H={L+L^*\over2},\qquad A={L-L^*\over2},\qquad \mathcal A=H^{-1/2}AH^{-1/2}.$$

The positive intrinsic skew frequencies are the positive singular values of the real skew matrix $\mathcal A$, one for each conjugate Fourier pair. With $1\le k<n/2$, set

$$a_k=\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}r_j\{1-\cos(2\pi kj/n)\},\qquad b_k=\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}r_j\sin(2\pi kj/n).$$
$$\boxed{\kappa_k={|b_k|\over a_k}}.$$

Irreducibility makes every $a_k$ positive. A possible real Fourier mode at $k=n/2$ has zero skew frequency; the discrete-Fourier diagonalization is the classical circulant construction in Davis (2012).

The small-cycle exclusion

$$5\le n\le8\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \#\{k:\kappa_k>1\}\le1.$$

For an orientation $\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}$ define the contribution of jump $j$ to the inequality $\epsilon b_k>a_k$ by

$$h_{k,\epsilon}(j)=\epsilon\sin(2\pi kj/n)-\{1-\cos(2\pi kj/n)\}.$$

If $\kappa_k>1$, one orientation has $\sum_jr_jh_{k,\epsilon}(j)>0$. To rule out two such modes it suffices, for every pair and both orientations, to find positive $c$ with

$$c\,h_{k,\epsilon}(j)+h_{\ell,\delta}(j)\le0 \quad\hbox{for every }j.$$

The certificate verifies all 150 jumpwise inequalities by outward rational bounds. It derives a rational enclosure of $\pi$ from Machin's formula and then uses alternating rational Taylor enclosures for sine and cosine, so the exclusions do not rest on floating rounding.

Why features cannot evade the full spectrum

Let $F$ be any centered $n\times4$ feature matrix of rank four and let $U=L^{-1}_0F$ be its centered Poisson potentials. Up to transposition, which only reverses the skew sign, its Green–Kubo block is

$$K=U^*LU,\qquad S=\operatorname{sym}K=U^*HU,\qquad D=\operatorname{skew}K=U^*AU.$$

After $H$-orthonormalizing the columns of $U$, the normalized feature skew matrix $S^{-1/2}DS^{-1/2}$ is a four-dimensional compression of $\mathcal A$. Apply Cauchy interlacing to the Hermitian matrix $i\mathcal A$: the second positive eigenvalue of the compression is at most the second positive eigenvalue of the full matrix.

Thus a full cyclic chain with at most one frequency above one cannot produce a four-feature block with two frequencies above one. This is a compression statement, not an inference from the largest frequency alone.

Application to the two-mode target

Consider the fixed-parameter population block

$$K_*=I_4+\cot(\pi/5)\operatorname{diag}(J,J),\qquad J=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

Its two positive intrinsic skew frequencies are both $\cot(\pi/5)>1$, since $\pi/5<\pi/4$. Therefore no translation-invariant chain on $5,6,7,$ or $8$ cyclic states realizes $K_*$, regardless of its nonnegative jump rates or the four centered features.

This does not settle the unrestricted minimum-state problem. It excludes an algebraically broad family that includes dense noncactus supports, while a non-translation-invariant overlapping-cycle generator could have different intrinsic geometry.

Certificate and scope

The certificate also checks 160 dense random circulant generators by constructing the full normalized skew matrix independently. Its Fourier frequencies agree with the matrix singular values to $5.2\times10^{-15}$, using a portable assertion tolerance of $2\times10^{-11}$.

The theorem concerns one fixed generator and one fixed feature block. It does not identify a hidden generator, convert rank after integrating maturity-dependent loadings into a regime count, or make a statement about marginal versus conditional conformal coverage.
