Even full occupation laws need not identify five states

Two strictly positive-rate five-state chains have the same stationary joint occupation-vector law at every fixed horizon, but are neither reversals nor relabelings. Five is the first possible state count when the stationary law and complete state representation are known.

The data: one horizon at a time

For an irreducible row generator $Q$, start in its known stationary law $\pi$ and observe the law of

$$L(T)=\left(\int_0^T\mathbf1_{\{Y_t=i\}}dt\right)_{i=1}^n\quad\text{for every }T\ge0.$$

This includes every mixed occupation cumulant at each horizon and every stationary integrated-covariance curve. It is not the joint law of $(L(T_1),L(T_2))$ at multiple horizons, the occupation process itself, or ordered observations.

A stationary block-reversal lemma

Let $A$ be $k\times k$, $B$ be $\ell\times\ell$, $a,d\in\mathbb R^k$, and suppose

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}A&a\mathbf1_\ell^T\\\mathbf1_\ell d^T&B\end{pmatrix} \quad\text{is an irreducible generator with uniform stationary law.}$$

Then the block-reversed matrix

$$\widetilde Q=\begin{pmatrix}A^T&d\mathbf1_\ell^T\\\mathbf1_\ell a^T&B\end{pmatrix}$$

is also a generator with the same uniform stationary law and the same fixed-horizon occupation laws. Row and column balance give $A\mathbf1=-\ell a$, $A^T\mathbf1=-\ell d$, $\mathbf1^Ta=\mathbf1^Td$, and $B\mathbf1=B^T\mathbf1=-(\mathbf1^Ta)\mathbf1$, which prove the generator assertions. The underlying undirected graph is unchanged; balance and connectedness imply irreducibility.

Proof including the stationary-start prefactor

For arbitrary real diagonal $D=\operatorname{diag}(D_A,D_B)$, the occupation transform is $M_Q(T,D)=\mathbf1^Te^{T(Q+D)}\mathbf1/(k+\ell)$. For sufficiently large real $z$, write

$$R=(zI-A-D_A)^{-1},\quad S=(zI-B-D_B)^{-1},$$ $$r=\mathbf1^TR\mathbf1,\quad s=\mathbf1^TS\mathbf1,\quad t=\mathbf1^TRa,\quad v=d^TR\mathbf1,\quad w=d^TRa.$$

Solving the two block equations for $(zI-Q-D)^{-1}\mathbf1$ gives

$$\int_0^\infty e^{-zT}M_Q(T,D)\,dT =\frac1{k+\ell}\left[r+\frac{s(1+t)(1+v)}{1-sw}\right].$$

Block reversal replaces $R$ by $R^T$ and swaps $a,d$; it therefore swaps $t,v$ and preserves $r,s,w$. The displayed scalar transform is unchanged. Uniqueness of the horizon Laplace transform gives equality of $M_Q(T,D)$ for every $T$, since it is continuous and of exponential order.

For each fixed $T$, $L(T)$ is bounded, so equality for all diagonal potentials identifies its joint law. This proves the finite-horizon statement, not merely equality of tilted eigenvalues or long-run cumulants.

An exact positive-rate five-state example

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}-5&1\\2&-7\end{pmatrix},\quad a=\begin{pmatrix}4/3\\5/3\end{pmatrix},\quad d=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix},\quad B=\begin{pmatrix}-11&4&4\\2&-10&5\\6&3&-12\end{pmatrix}.$$

Both assembled generators have minimum off-diagonal rate $1$ and uniform stationary law on five states. Their common diagonal $(-5,-7,-11,-10,-12)$ has distinct entries, so a relabeling between them must be the identity. They are unequal, and $B\ne B^T$ excludes global reversal, even after relabeling.

Ordered observations distinguish them: with the state labels above,

$$\mathbb P_Q(Y_0=1,Y_t=2)-\mathbb P_{\widetilde Q}(Y_0=1,Y_t=2) =-t/5+O(t^2),\qquad t\downarrow0.$$

The coefficient follows directly from $(Q_{12}-\widetilde Q_{12})/5$. At $t=0.1$, the matrix exponentials give a difference of $-0.0094670253$ to the shown precision.

Why four states cannot do this

Now allow any known positive $\pi$, not just uniform. On the centered space put $H=(\Pi-Q)^{-1}-\Pi$, $G=(H+H^*)/2$, $K=(H-H^*)/2$, with $\Pi=\mathbf1\pi$ and $*$ the $\pi$-weighted adjoint.

Define the occupation covariance operator by $\langle f,C(T)h\rangle_\pi=\operatorname{Cov}_\pi(\int_0^T f(Y_t)dt,\int_0^T h(Y_t)dt)$. With $B_0=(H^2+(H^*)^2)/2$, twice integrating the stationary semigroup gives the exact identity

$$C(T)=2TG-2B_0+H^2e^{TQ}+(H^2e^{TQ})^*=2TG-2B_0+o(1).$$

Thus the data determine $G$, $B_0$ and $K^2=B_0-G^2$ exactly. The remainder tends to zero for every fixed finite irreducible chain, including defective generators.

In a weighted orthonormal centered basis of dimension at most three, a real skew matrix is determined by its square up to sign. In dimension three write $Kx=b\times x$; then $K^2=bb^T-\|b\|^2I$, so $bb^T=K^2-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(K^2)I$ determines $b$ up to sign. Dimensions one and two are immediate, with the zero case giving no ambiguity.

Hence for $n\le4$ the data determine $H=G+K$ up to weighted adjunction, and $Q=\Pi-(\Pi+H)^{-1}$ up to stationary time reversal. The five-state example makes this threshold sharp for universal identification.

Weighted transposition gives $M_{Q^*}(T,D)=M_Q(T,D)$ for $Q^*=D_\pi^{-1}Q^TD_\pi$, so reversal really is invisible to these stationary occupation laws.

Classical mechanism, certificate and scope

Block transposition across a rank-one cut is a known principal-minor-preserving operation; see Chatterjee, Ghosh, Gurjar and Raj (2025), Definition 2.10 and Lemma 2.12. The proof above additionally tracks the stationary scalar resolvent needed for occupation laws; principal-minor equality of $Q$ alone is not used as a substitute.

The certificate checks 62 principal-minor identities for both $Q$ and $Q-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T$ against their partners. Principal-minor expansion and the determinant lemma then certify the numerator and denominator of $\mathbf1^T(zI-Q-D)^{-1}\mathbf1$ as polynomials in arbitrary diagonal variables.

It also verifies 24 rational block-resolvent identities, all 240 relabeling/reversal exclusions, and the common full Gram and covariance boundary exactly. Independent floating-point checks compare 544 finite-horizon transforms across 17 block pairs; these are not interval enclosures.

The indistinguishability requires the specified stationary start and time-homogeneous occupation totals. It does not cover arbitrary initial priors, joint observations across horizons, time-weighted functionals, or ordered data. The theorem assumes known state labels and time unit; it is not a claim about partial features, statistical estimation or rank after integrating different maturity loadings.
