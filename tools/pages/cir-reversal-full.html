An exact CIR cycle-reversal profile

With common mean reversion, a stationary cycle-direction effect has the same maturity profile at every switching speed.

Let $Q$ be a finite irreducible row generator with stationary law $\pi$, and $Q^\dagger=D_\pi^{-1}Q^\top D_\pi$ its stationary reversal, where $D_\pi=\operatorname{diag}\pi$. Compare regime generators $mQ$ and $mQ^\dagger$ in

$$dv_t=\kappa(\theta_{Y_t}-v_t)dt+\sqrt{a_{Y_t}v_t}\,dB_t,\qquad V_T=\int_0^T v_tdt.$$

Here $\kappa>0$ is common, $\theta_i,a_i>0$, $2\kappa\theta_i\ge a_i$, and $B$ is independent of the autonomous regime chain. Each model starts in its own joint invariant law for $(v,Y)$, separately at every speed. This is neither a common deterministic $v_0$ nor reversal of the entire joint diffusion.

Write $\mu=\pi\theta$, $f=\theta-\mu\mathbf1$, $g=a-(\pi a)\mathbf1$ and $\langle u,w\rangle_\pi=\sum_i\pi_i u_iw_i$. Define

$$R_\kappa(m)=(\kappa I-mQ)^{-1},\qquad A_\kappa(m)=\tfrac12\{\langle f,R_\kappa(m)g\rangle_\pi-\langle g,R_\kappa(m)f\rangle_\pi\},$$ $$L_\kappa(T)=2\int_0^T(T-t)e^{-\kappa t}dt =\frac{2(\kappa T-1+e^{-\kappa T})}{\kappa^2}.$$

Exact factorization at all maturities

Let $\Delta$ denote forward minus reversed-regime values, always with the corresponding invariant initialization. Then, for every $m>0$ and $T\ge0$,

$$\boxed{\Delta\operatorname{Var}(v)=A_\kappa(m),\qquad \Delta\operatorname{Var}(V_T)=L_\kappa(T)A_\kappa(m).}$$ $$\Delta\!\left\{\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{Var}(V_T)}T\right\} =\frac{2}{\kappa}A_\kappa(m).$$

Since $L_\kappa(T)>0$ for $T>0$, the sign of this reversal gap cannot change with maturity at fixed speed. Constant $a$, or more generally $a=b_0\mathbf1+bf$, makes the gap zero at every speed and maturity. A zero gap does not by itself establish reversibility or determine the complete variance-process law.

For independent-price Brownian returns $M_T=\sqrt{V_T}Z$, with independent standard normal $Z$, the fourth-cumulant gap is exactly $3L_\kappa(T)A_\kappa(m)$. This is not a fourth-cumulant formula for drifted log returns or leveraged price noise.

Proof: the covariance gap comes from the one-time variance

The stationary first-moment row is $r=\kappa\pi D_\theta R_\kappa(m)$, where $D_\theta=\operatorname{diag}\theta$, and $\mathbb Ev=\mu$. The second-moment identity gives

$$\operatorname{Var}(v)=\frac{(\pi a)\mu}{2\kappa} +\kappa\langle f,R_\kappa(m)f\rangle_\pi+\tfrac12\langle f,R_\kappa(m)g\rangle_\pi.$$

Under regime reversal, the resolvent becomes its weighted adjoint. The first two terms are unchanged and the last cross pairing is transposed, proving the one-time gap.

For $C_v(t)=\operatorname{Cov}(v_0,v_t)$, conditional expectation of the CIR equation, followed by stationary covariance with $v_0$, yields

$$C_v(t)=e^{-\kappa t}\operatorname{Var}(v) +\kappa^2\int_0^t e^{-\kappa(t-u)} \langle f,R_\kappa(m)e^{muQ}f\rangle_\pi du.$$

The integral is unchanged under regime reversal: the resolvent and semigroup commute, and taking the weighted adjoint transposes their scalar quadratic pairing. Thus $\Delta C_v(t)=e^{-\kappa t}A_\kappa(m)$. Integrating $2\int_0^T(T-t)C_v(t)dt$ proves the finite-maturity identity, and covariance integrability gives the long-run identity.

The Green–Kubo limit, with its precise uniform scope

Assume $\|e^{tQ}-\mathbf1\pi\|_\infty\le Ce^{-\gamma t}$ with a justified $C,\gamma>0$, and put $H=-Q^\#$.

$$K^{\rm anti}_{fa}=\tfrac12\{\langle f,Hg\rangle_\pi-\langle g,Hf\rangle_\pi\},$$ $$\sup_{T>0}\left|\frac{\Delta\operatorname{Var}(V_T)}{L_\kappa(T)} -\frac{K^{\rm anti}_{fa}}m\right| \le\frac{\kappa C\|f\|_\infty\|g\|_\infty}{m^2\gamma^2}.$$

Indeed the resolvent integral differs from $H/m$ by the factor $e^{-\kappa t}-1$, whose magnitude is at most $\kappa t$. Apply the semigroup bound to both centered cross pairings. This is uniform for the normalized profile, not an absolute maturity-uniform error after truncating a growing variance slope.

A strict-Feller three-state witness

Take the unit directed three-cycle, $\kappa=1$, $\theta=(0.03,0.04,0.05)$ and $a=(0.02,0.04,0.01)$. The minimum Feller margin is $1/25$, and exact symbolic calculation gives

$$A_1(m)=-\frac{m}{12000(1+3m+3m^2)},\qquad K^{\rm anti}_{fa}=-\frac1{36000}.$$

For every positive speed and maturity the forward integrated-variance variance is strictly smaller. At $m=T=1$ the gap is $-8.7590343\times10^{-6}$, and the independent-return fourth-cumulant gap is $-2.6277103\times10^{-5}$.

When first-order blindness is exact, and when it is not

For an $n$-state chain with $n\ge2$, define $K_j=\tfrac12\{\langle f,H^jg\rangle_\pi-\langle g,H^jf\rangle_\pi\}$, so $K_1=K^{\rm anti}_{fa}$. Then $A_\kappa(m)=0$ for every $m>0$ if and only if $K_j=0$ for $1\le j\le n-2$. The empty condition for two states agrees with automatic reversibility.

To prove this, restrict $H$ to the $(n-1)$-dimensional centered space and expand $R_\kappa(m)=m^{-1}H(I+\kappa H/m)^{-1}$ for sufficiently large $m$. Necessity follows by uniqueness of its convergent power series, while Cayley–Hamilton reduces every higher power to $I,H,\ldots,H^{n-2}$, whose antisymmetric scalar pairings vanish under the stated condition. The resulting zero rational function vanishes at every positive speed, since the resolvent has no pole there.

Thus for at most three states, $K_1=0$ implies exact blindness at every speed and maturity; four states are the smallest possible exception. Under the same semigroup envelope as above, the next-order expansion has the explicit bound

$$\left|A_\kappa(m)-\frac{K_1}{m}+\frac{\kappa K_2}{m^2}\right| \le\frac{\kappa^2 C\|f\|_\infty\|g\|_\infty}{m^3\gamma^3},\qquad m>0.$$

Indeed $H^2=\int_0^\infty t(e^{tQ}-\mathbf1\pi)dt$, and $|e^{-x}-1+x|\le x^2/2$ for $x\ge0$ bounds the two resolvent pairings. As before this bounds the normalized maturity profile, not an absolute error uniform in $T$.

A rational four-state example has uniform $\pi$, $\kappa=1$ and

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}-6&2&1&3\\1&-4&2&1\\2&1&-4&1\\3&1&1&-5\end{pmatrix},\qquad \theta=\tfrac1{100}(11,9,10,10),\quad a=\tfrac1{200}(8,12,11,17).$$

All off-diagonal rates are positive, the minimum Feller margin is $23/200$, and exact arithmetic gives

$$K_1=0,\qquad K_2=\frac1{18880000},\qquad A_1(m)=-\frac{m}{80000(236m^3+117m^2+19m+1)}.$$

This gap is strictly negative at every positive speed and maturity despite the vanishing first-order signal, and $m^2A_1(m)\to-1/18880000$. It is a limitation of a prescribed observable pair, not a claim that the full Green–Kubo operator misses irreversibility.

Certificate and scope

The certificate independently evolves centered degree-two joint moments in 840 profile checks, with 168 affine Poisson comparisons, 168 finite-rate bounds and 168 affine-loading blindness controls on random, cyclic and defective chains. It proves both displayed amplitudes in symbolic rational arithmetic, checks the low-state power reduction, and adds 20 independent four-state moment comparisons and 173 next-order bounds.

The polynomial moment framework is classical; see Cuchiero, Keller-Ressel and Teichmann (2012). The result is an exact stationary second-cumulant specialization, not a new moment method, a finite-speed monotonicity theorem, or a statement with switching mean reversion.
