Norm-flat covariance

A dual-norm Green–Kubo maximum gives the universal geometric constant.

The radial and ellipsoidal flat-map constants extend to every fixed norm, including non-smooth polyhedral norms. The theorem depends on a dual quadratic maximum, not on Green–Kubo rank alone.

The theorem

Use the finite-chain setup of the radial theorem, with centered $g:\mathsf S\to\mathbb R^d$, fixed $T>0$, occupation average $W_{m,T}$, and nonzero symmetric Green–Kubo matrix $\Gamma$. Let $N$ be any norm on $\mathbb R^d$, with dual norm

$$N_*(\ell)=\sup_{N(w)\le1}\ell^\top w.$$

Define the dual Green–Kubo magnitude

$$\Lambda_N(\Gamma) =\sup_{N_*(\ell)\le1}\ell^\top\Gamma\ell =\|\Gamma^{1/2}\|_{2\to N}^2>0.$$

For $\sigma>1$, $b>0$ and $w\ne0$, put

$$\Phi_{\sigma,b,N}(w)=\exp\{-\exp(bN(w)^{-1/(\sigma-1)})\}, \qquad \Phi_{\sigma,b,N}(0)=0.$$

At every fixed $T>0$, uniformly over arbitrary initial regime priors,

$$-\log\operatorname{Var}(\Phi_{\sigma,b,N}(W_{m,T})) \sim\frac{b^{2(\sigma-1)}}{2\Lambda_N(\Gamma)} \frac{mT}{[\log(mT)]^{2(\sigma-1)}}.$$

Norm boundary contraction

Put $\beta=\sigma-1$ and $H=\operatorname{range}\Gamma$. The moderate-deviation rate on $H$ is $J(y)=b^{2\beta}y^\top\Gamma^+y/2$.

Writing $y=\Gamma^{1/2}z$ on $H$ turns the norm boundary into

$$\min_{y\in H:\,N(y)=1}J(y) =\frac{b^{2\beta}}2 \min_{z\in H:\,N(\Gamma^{1/2}z)=1}\|z\|_2^2 =\frac{b^{2\beta}}{2\|\Gamma^{1/2}\|_{2\to N}^2}.$$

Operator-norm duality gives the displayed formula for $\Lambda_N(\Gamma)$. The shrinking-boundary and variance-separation arguments then follow the radial proof; differentiability or strict convexity of $N$ is not required.

Useful special cases

For the Euclidean norm, $\Lambda_N(\Gamma)=\lambda_{\max}(\Gamma)$. For $N(w)=(w^\top A w)^{1/2}$, it is $\lambda_{\max}(\Gamma^{1/2}A\Gamma^{1/2})$, recovering the ellipsoidal theorem.

For $N=\|\cdot\|_\infty$, duality and positive semidefiniteness give $\Lambda_N(\Gamma)=\max_i\Gamma_{ii}$. For $N=\|\cdot\|_1$,

$$\Lambda_N(\Gamma)=\max_{s\in\{-1,1\}^d}s^\top\Gamma s.$$

Coordinate and rank scope

Under $w'=Rw$, transport the norm as $N'(w')=N(R^{-1}w')$ and take $\Gamma'=R\Gamma R^\top$. Its dual obeys $N'_*(\ell')=N_*(R^\top\ell')$, so $\Lambda_{N'}(\Gamma')=\Lambda_N(\Gamma)$.

This is a fixed-feature geometric magnitude. It neither identifies a post-averaging nonlinear covariance rank nor combines maturity-dependent loadings; $\operatorname{rank}\Gamma$ still records only the active linear feature space.

Numerical certificate

For the nonreversible three-state two-feature chain used in the radial checks, take $N=\|\cdot\|_1$, $\sigma=2$ and $b=1.5$. The certificate finds $\Lambda_N(\Gamma)=1.508362735$ at sign $(-1,-1)$ and predicted constant $0.745841815$.

The exact two-dimensional Legendre rate is contracted over the non-smooth $\ell_1$ diamond. At norm radius $.005$, its quadratic normalization is $.999690067$; transporting the norm through a non-orthogonal coordinate map changes the dual constant by less than the printed precision.

Literature boundary
